# Resolutive Science v0.4.0 — Post-Falsification Development Plan

**Status:** preregistered development plan  
**Parent release:** v0.3.0 — DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22643804`  
**Scientific baseline:** RSMS-1.0-rc.1 / frozen RCMS v0.3 record

## 1. Purpose

v0.4 begins only after publication of the v0.3 falsification record. It does not rewrite, rescue, or reinterpret the failed P16 prediction. The v0.3 logarithmic surrogate and all Protocols 01–16 remain immutable historical evidence.

The purpose of v0.4 is **model diagnosis before model extension**: determine which structural assumptions of the one-parameter RCMS surrogate fail across probes, redshift ranges, and radial/transverse observables, and establish whether any next-generation resolutive cosmology is justified.

## 2. Frozen facts inherited from v0.3

The development line shall treat the following as fixed evidence:

1. null and injection/recovery calibration did not reveal a material intrinsic positive bias in the tested estimator;
2. the positive descriptive direction was distributed across previously analyzed dataset families;
3. the logarithmic deformation was functionally non-specific against equally complex smooth controls;
4. the fitted amplitude was sensitive to analysis channel/systematics;
5. a universal amplitude was not established across families;
6. complexity penalties did not establish preference over Lambda-CDM; and
7. the preregistered positive BOSS DR12 prediction failed within the frozen primary domain and reached the negative `A_R=-1` boundary.

No v0.4 analysis may erase or relabel these outcomes.

## 3. Governing rule

**Diagnosis precedes extension.**

No new free physical parameter, redshift law, coupling, or dataset-specific correction may be promoted into RCMS merely because it improves the v0.3 residuals. Candidate extensions must be motivated independently, preregistered, and tested against held-out information.

Exploratory work shall be labeled exploratory and shall not be counted as confirmatory evidence.

## 4. Planned protocol sequence

### P17 — Residual topology and sign map

Reconstruct the signed residual structure of the frozen Lambda-CDM and RCMS fits by dataset family, redshift, and observable channel. The goal is to determine whether the v0.3 heterogeneity is random, redshift-organized, or radial/transverse organized.

Primary outputs:
- signed normalized residuals;
- family/redshift/channel maps;
- covariance-aware residual correlations;
- no refitting of a new physical model.

### P18 — Radial/transverse consistency audit

Test the P13 radial-versus-transverse tension across every dataset for which both channels can be represented consistently. Determine whether the DESI DR2 sign reversal is isolated or recurrent.

### P19 — Redshift locality and change-point diagnostics

Without introducing a new cosmological law, test whether the inferred deformation is statistically compatible with a constant amplitude over redshift or shows evidence of locality/change points. Any change-point search is diagnostic only and incurs an explicit look-elsewhere penalty.

### P20 — Cross-survey concordance at overlapping redshift

Compare independent surveys in overlapping or nearby redshift windows. This protocol asks whether disagreement follows cosmic epoch or survey/instrument family.

### P21 — Minimal-extension candidate generation

Only after P17–P20 are frozen may candidate next-generation forms be proposed. Every candidate must:
- have an explicit mathematical/physical motivation;
- declare parameter count and dimensional status;
- reduce to a defined baseline limit;
- avoid dataset-specific switches;
- be compared against equally complex non-resolutive controls.

This stage is exploratory and cannot validate a candidate.

### P22 — Candidate calibration and identifiability

For any surviving P21 candidate, run null calibration, injection/recovery, identifiability, boundary, and parameter-degeneracy tests before observational model selection.

### P23 — Frozen comparative evaluation

Compare preregistered candidates against Lambda-CDM and equal-complexity controls using AIC/BIC and, where computationally justified and prior-robust, Bayesian evidence or predictive scoring.

### P24 — New held-out prediction

The v0.4 publication gate. A target not used to construct or rank the candidate shall be selected and frozen before inspection. Prediction, domain, sign/direction, uncertainty band, failure rules, and boundary rules must be preregistered.

A failed P24 prediction shall be published as such and shall not trigger post-hoc rescue within the same release line.

## 5. Release gates

A v0.4 scientific release candidate requires:

- P17–P20 completed and frozen;
- any P21 candidate generation clearly separated from confirmatory tests;
- P22 calibration completed for every candidate entering P23;
- P23 complexity-aware comparison completed;
- P24 held-out prediction completed;
- favorable and unfavorable outcomes preserved;
- no rewrite of v0.3 tags, results, DOI, or interpretation.

If P17–P20 show that the signal is adequately explained by survey/channel heterogeneity with no stable cross-survey structure, v0.4 may close as a diagnostic negative release without proposing a new RCMS form.

## 6. Scientific status

This plan does not claim discovery, experimental validation, or evidence for a new physical law. Its purpose is to convert the falsification information from v0.3 into discriminating tests. The correct outcome may be a new candidate, retention of Lambda-CDM, or termination of the current RCMS cosmological surrogate line.
