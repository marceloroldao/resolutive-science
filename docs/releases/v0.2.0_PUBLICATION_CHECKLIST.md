## 7. Claims boundary required for v0.2.0

The release MUST NOT claim that:

1. Resolutive Physics has been experimentally validated;
2. RCMS is statistically preferred over Lambda-CDM;
3. the recurring positive `A_R` direction is a discovery significance;
4. the logarithmic term has been derived from a fundamental Resolutive Master Equation;
5. Protocol 08's scale-composition hypothesis has been physically established.

The release MAY state, with the protocol qualifications preserved, that:

1. multiple frozen observational screenings repeatedly select a positive RCMS deformation direction;
2. DESI DR2 independently reproduces that positive direction with a best-fit amplitude close to the earlier low-redshift result;
3. the preregistered descriptive synthesis is compatible with a common positive amplitude near `A_R ~ 0.21` without treating overlapping evidence as independent discovery evidence;
4. the logarithmic form has a conditional mathematical derivation from continuous additive drift under multiplicative scale composition;
5. robustness and model-selection limitations remain part of the permanent evidence record.
