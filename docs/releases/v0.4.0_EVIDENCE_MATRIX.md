## Key numerical diagnostics

Under RCMS:

- DESI radial-minus-transverse canonical residual-mean gap: about `-0.353 sigma`;
- BOSS radial-minus-transverse gap: about `+0.207 sigma`;
- DESI low-minus-high redshift gap: about `-0.182 sigma`;
- BOSS low-minus-high redshift gap: about `+0.074 sigma`.

All remain below the preregistered organized-tension threshold.

## Cumulative diagnosis

The diagnostic block rejects several simple rescue narratives:

1. **Not a simple residual channel pattern:** P17 is diffuse after full-vector fitting.
2. **Not a universal radial/transverse effect:** P18 finds the strong split only in DESI.
3. **Not a simple redshift transition:** P19 finds no calibrated locality.
4. **Not explained by cosmic epoch alone:** P20 shows discordance at the same redshift.

The evidence therefore does not identify a stable cross-survey organizing law suitable for preregistered candidate construction.

## Development decision

`DIAGNOSTIC_STOP_NO_EXTENSION`

P21–P24 are not activated. They are not counted as failed tests; their prerequisite, a scientifically motivated candidate emerging from P17–P20, is absent.

## Scientific boundary

v0.4 does not establish that Lambda-CDM is complete, nor that all Resolutive Cosmology is impossible. It establishes a narrower result: **the current one-parameter RCMS evidence does not support a defensible data-driven extension based on channel or redshift structure.**

The appropriate next step is an independent likelihood/data-convention/systematics audit rather than adding parameters to the cosmological surrogate.
