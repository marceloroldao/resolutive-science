# Resolutive Science v0.4.0 — Diagnostic Gate Decision

**Status:** FROZEN DEVELOPMENT DECISION  
**Decision:** `DIAGNOSTIC_STOP_NO_EXTENSION`  
**Parent release:** v0.3.0 — DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22643804`

## Decision

The diagnosis-before-extension block P17–P20 is complete. The preregistered stop condition in `v0.4.0_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` is activated.

**No P21 cosmological candidate generation will be performed in the v0.4 line.**

This is not a claim that every possible Resolutive Cosmology is falsified. It is a decision that the currently observed inconsistencies do not provide a stable, cross-survey empirical structure from which a new RCMS functional form can be defensibly derived without post-hoc overfitting.

## Evidence supporting the gate

- P17 — `DIFFUSE_RESIDUAL_STRUCTURE`: after full-vector fitting, residuals do not show a preregistered strong channel or redshift topology.
- P18 — `SURVEY_SPECIFIC_CHANNEL_TENSION`: the DESI radial/transverse amplitude sign reversal is not replicated by BOSS, whose channel subsets are weakly identifying.
- P19 — `NO_REDSHIFT_LOCALITY_EVIDENCE`: calibrated redshift split scans do not support a redshift-local transition.
- P20 — `EXACT_Z_SURVEY_DISCORDANCE`: at exactly `z=0.51`, DESI and BOSS pull the frozen surrogate in opposite directions with non-overlapping local profiles; BOSS is boundary-limited.

## Why an extension is not justified

A defensible new cosmological term would require a reproducible organizing feature: for example, a recurrent channel dependence, a calibrated redshift dependence, or a consistent cross-survey direction. P17–P20 provide none of these simultaneously.

Creating a redshift switch, radial/transverse coefficient, survey offset, or extra amplitude now would therefore be response-dependent model construction from the same data that exposed the failure.

That is explicitly disallowed by the v0.4 development rule: diagnosis precedes extension.

## Consequence for P21–P24

P21–P24 are **not failed protocols**. They are **not activated** because their prerequisite — a scientifically motivated candidate emerging from P17–P20 — is absent.

The v0.4 release can therefore close after P20 as a diagnostic negative release.

## Recommended next research line

Any later cosmology work should begin in a new version and first investigate non-model explanations for the cross-survey discordance, including:

- exact BAO observable/likelihood convention matching;
- covariance and compression choices;
- survey-specific nuisance/calibration structure;
- independent BAO datasets not used to formulate the current diagnosis;
- reproduction with external cosmology software or an independent implementation.

Only if a stable structure survives such audits should a new RCMS candidate be preregistered.

## Preservation rule

No v0.3 or v0.4 tag, outcome, prediction interval, boundary, classification, or DOI may be rewritten to accommodate a later model.
