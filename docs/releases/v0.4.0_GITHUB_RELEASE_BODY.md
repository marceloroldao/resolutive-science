# Resolutive Science v0.4.0 — Post-Falsification Diagnostic Release

## Summary

v0.4.0 is a diagnostic negative release following the v0.3 falsification campaign. It keeps the one-parameter RCMS surrogate unchanged and tests whether the observed inconsistencies organize into a reproducible channel, redshift, or cross-survey structure.

Frozen surrogate:

\[
E^2(z)=\Omega_m(1+z)^3+(1-\Omega_m)+A_R\ln(1+z).
\]

No new cosmological parameter or functional form is introduced.

## Protocols added

- **P17** — residual topology and sign map;
- **P18** — radial/transverse parameter-consistency audit;
- **P19** — redshift-locality diagnostic with preregistered look-elsewhere calibration;
- **P20** — exact-redshift DESI/BOSS cross-survey concordance.

## Frozen outcomes

- P17: `DIFFUSE_RESIDUAL_STRUCTURE`;
- P18: `SURVEY_SPECIFIC_CHANNEL_TENSION`;
- P19: `NO_REDSHIFT_LOCALITY_EVIDENCE`;
- P20: `EXACT_Z_SURVEY_DISCORDANCE`.

At the exact matched redshift `z=0.51`:

- DESI local `A_R=+0.160`, profile `[+0.08,+0.25]`;
- BOSS local `A_R=-1.000`, profile `[-1.00,-0.89]`;
- signs are opposite and intervals do not overlap;
- BOSS is boundary-limited.

## Development decision

The preregistered stop condition is activated:

`DIAGNOSTIC_STOP_NO_EXTENSION`

P21–P24 are not activated because P17–P20 do not yield a stable cross-survey organizing structure from which a new RCMS form can be defensibly motivated without response-dependent overfitting.

This is not a claim that every possible Resolutive Cosmology is falsified. It is a reproducible decision not to extend the current surrogate from the same residuals that exposed its inconsistency.

## Scientific interpretation

The diagnostic campaign rejects several simple rescue narratives:

- no strong post-fit channel/redshift residual topology survives P17;
- the DESI radial/transverse split is not independently replicated by BOSS;
- no calibrated redshift change-point appears in P19;
- DESI and BOSS disagree even at the same redshift in P20.

The appropriate next research line is therefore an independent likelihood/data-convention/systematics audit before any new cosmological candidate is proposed.

v0.4.0 does not claim discovery of new physics, experimental validation of Resolutive Physics, or statistical preference of RCMS over Lambda-CDM.
