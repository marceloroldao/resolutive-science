# Chapter 7 — Observables, Projection, and Measurement

## 7.1 Normative scope

This chapter specializes the observable space and observable projection operator
introduced in RS-D006 and RS-O001. It does not replace those canonical
definitions, prescribe dynamics, or assert that a resolutive hypothesis has been
empirically validated.

## 7.2 Observable projection

### RS-P100 — Observable projection framework

RS-P100 is a framework for specializations of the observable projection operator
\(\Pi\) canonically defined by RS-O001. Every specialization shall
cross-reference RS-O001, retain its domain and codomain unless an explicitly
identified restriction is declared, and state all additional mathematical
assumptions and physical-status labels. RS-P100 does not create a second
normative definition of \(\Pi\).

RS-F009 supplies the corresponding operator-signature specialization. Neither
RS-F009 nor RS-P100 changes the ownership, meaning, or stable identifier of
RS-O001.

## 7.3 Validation classification

### RS-M113 — Validation categories and scientific acceptance

Validation categories shall be reported as applicable classifications, not as a
mandatory linear sequence. Mathematical validation, numerical validation, and
empirical validation address distinct claims and may be pursued, satisfied,
revised, or rejected without implying that one category automatically
establishes another.

Independent reproduction is an additional relation to a reported result and may
apply independently to a mathematical, numerical, or empirical result. A report
shall identify what was reproduced, by whom or by what independent
implementation or analysis, and under which declared assumptions and
tolerances.

Scientific acceptance is a broader judgment of the scientific community. It is
not a technical validation stage, does not follow automatically from any
validation category or reproduction result, and cannot be self-declared by an
author, model, implementation, or project.
