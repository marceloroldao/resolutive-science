# v0.5 T26 — Geometry Diagnostic Protocol

**Object:** frozen T25 coordinate-free endogenous generator.
**Rule:** no generator parameter may be changed in T26.

## Question

Why did T25 produce disagreement among growth, spectral and walk dimensions?

T26 diagnoses the graph before any second relational law is proposed.

## Measurements

1. edges and connected components;
2. cycle rank (first graph Betti number): beta_1 = E - V + C;
3. beta_1/N;
4. triangle count;
5. degree histogram;
6. sampled diameter/eccentricity;
7. root-resolved ball-volume slopes;
8. lazy-random-walk return probability;
9. scale-local spectral dimension.

The lazy walk is P_lazy=(I+P)/2. It is used only to remove parity oscillation from bipartite/tree-like graphs; it is an estimator regularization, not new physical dynamics.

## Decision gates

### Tree-like diagnosis
If beta_1/N -> 0 and short-cycle density remains negligible, classify the family as asymptotically tree-like/sparse for this diagnostic.

### Stable polynomial geometry
Requires a root-robust plateau in local d_g(r) that expands with N before saturation.

### Stable spectral geometry
Requires a time-window plateau in local d_s(t) that expands with N before the stationary floor.

### Single effective dimension
Requires compatible stable scaling sectors. Numerical coincidence at one N or one window is insufficient.

## Forbidden actions

- changing max_degree or sample_size;
- adding edges after seeing T25;
- selecting windows solely because they approach 3;
- identifying d_s, d_g, d_w or z_R with one another by definition;
- claiming physical spatial dimension.
