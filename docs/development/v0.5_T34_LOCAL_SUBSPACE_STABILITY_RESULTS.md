**Protocol:** `v0.5_T34_LOCAL_SUBSPACE_STABILITY_PROTOCOL.md`  
**Parent:** T33. **Dynamics:** none.  
**Matrix:** N=128,256,512; seeds 11,23,47; R=1,2,4,8.

## 1. Executive result

T34 finds a strongly concentrated local singular spectrum, but it does **not** derive a physical tangent-space dimension.

Two effects must be separated:

1. the rank distribution is strongly constrained by the tree-like parent graph, whose degree distribution is concentrated at degree 1–3 with mean degree approaching 2;
2. the preregistered R->2R principal-angle diagnostic is exactly unity by construction because the smaller profile is the coordinate prefix of the larger profile.

Therefore the apparent cross-scale subspace agreement cannot be interpreted as emergent geometry.

## 5. Cross-scale principal-angle audit

The frozen protocol compares X_R(v) with the first R+1 columns of X_2R(v).

But the profile definition is nested exactly:

```text
p_R(v) = p_2R(v)[0:R+1].
```

Therefore for every edge v->w,

```text
delta p_R(v->w)
=
delta p_2R(v->w)[0:R+1].
```

Hence

```text
X_R(v) = X_2R(v)[:,0:R+1]
```

identically.

Their row spaces are therefore the same whenever nonzero. The measured principal cosines are 1 to floating-point precision for every tested N and transition:

```text
1->2 : mean principal cosine = 1.000000
2->4 : mean principal cosine = 1.000000
4->8 : mean principal cosine = 1.000000
```

This is not empirical scale stability. It is an algebraic consequence of the diagnostic definition.

Therefore:

```text
T34_SCALE_SUBSPACE_STABILITY =
    NONDISCRIMINATING_BY_CONSTRUCTION
```

The corresponding gate is not promoted to IDENTIFIED.

## 6. Low-rank classification

The participation rank is substantially below the available exact rank for R>=2, so a descriptive singular-spectrum concentration is present.

However the result is representation- and degree-conditioned:

- the exact rank tracks local graph degree;
- the participation rank is dominated by a first singular direction;
- the cumulative radial profile induces strongly correlated coordinates;
- the scale-overlap test is tautological under prefix restriction.

Therefore the conservative classification is:

```text
T34_LOW_RANK_STRUCTURE =
    IDENTIFIED_DESCRIPTIVE_REPRESENTATION_CONDITIONED
```

not a physical tangent-space claim.

## 9. Consequence

T34 closes without promoting dimension or local tangent geometry.

A future scale test must compare local subspaces in a common higher-dimensional ambient space without truncating the higher-scale profile back to the lower-scale coordinates. One mathematically non-tautological option is the canonical zero-padding inclusion

```text
E_R : R^(R+1) -> R^(2R+1),
E_R(x) = (x,0,...,0),
```

followed by principal-angle comparison between

```text
rowspace(E_R X_R)
```

and

```text
rowspace(X_2R).
```

That proposal is not executed here and requires a separately frozen protocol.
