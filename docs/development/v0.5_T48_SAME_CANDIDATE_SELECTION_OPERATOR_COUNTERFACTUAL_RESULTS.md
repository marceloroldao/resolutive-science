## 7. Mathematical mechanism

At birth v, let C_v be the sampled set of older candidate vertices.

The frozen T25 selection is

```text
p(v)
=
arg min_{u in C_v, degree(u)<6}
[degree_t(u), tie(u)]
```

followed by the edge

```text
(v,p(v)).
```

The update is local:

- finite candidate sample;
- recursive newer-to-older attachment;
- bounded coordination;
- preference for lower current local load;
- no global graph metric in the selection rule.

## 8. Interpretation boundary

T48 identifies a robust regulatory mechanism inside the mathematical model.

It does not establish that nature samples five candidates, minimizes graph degree, or obeys this exact update rule.

A physical theory still requires a mapping from candidate set, local load and update event to measurable physical quantities.
