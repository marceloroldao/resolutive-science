**Status:** Development roadmap

## Purpose

The v0.4 development line moves one level upstream from the frozen RCMS phenomenological cosmology surrogate. The purpose is not to repair, widen, or retune the v0.3 cosmological fit. Its purpose is to determine whether a smaller set of resolutive primitive assumptions can generate cross-domain dynamics and falsifiable observables without post-hoc rescue.

The v0.3.0 RCMS falsification record remains immutable. In particular, the failed preregistered BOSS DR12 positive prediction and its frozen-domain boundary outcome are not revised in this development line.

## Core scientific question

Can a compact resolutive dynamical structure generate independently testable consequences across more than one physical domain while preserving dimensional consistency, limiting behavior, computational implementability, and symmetric comparison against established models?

## Classification discipline

Every candidate statement introduced in v0.4 shall be placed in exactly one of the following classes before it is used in a numerical test.

### D — Derived

A statement is **derived** only when it follows from previously declared definitions, mathematical assumptions, and equations through explicit steps that can be independently checked.

Required record:

- source definitions and equation identifiers;
- derivation steps;
- assumptions used;
- dimensional check;
- limiting cases;
- implementation mapping, if applicable.

### P — Postulated

A statement is **postulated** when it is introduced as an independent hypothesis rather than obtained from prior structure.

Required record:

- exact mathematical statement;
- physical interpretation;
- why the postulate is needed;
- free parameters introduced;
- known degeneracies;
- conditions that would falsify or restrict it.

### F — Falsifiable prediction

A statement is a **falsifiable prediction** only when its observable, domain, parameter treatment, comparison model, acceptance/failure criterion, and data or experiment are specified before evaluation.

Required record:

- predicted observable;
- numerical or directional prediction;
- uncertainty or allowed interval where applicable;
- target dataset/experiment;
- nuisance treatment;
- frozen parameter domain;
- success/failure rule;
- boundary rule;
- no post-hoc rescue clause.

## Fundamental-dynamics audit

The first v0.4 task is an audit of the candidate Resolutive Master Equation and its surrounding concepts. No candidate equation is normative merely because it appeared in prior research discussions.

The audit shall classify each element as derived, postulated, provisional, computational, or observational.

Candidate concepts to audit include, where mathematically available:

- resolutive state and state relation;
- resolutive density and its dimensional meaning;
- phase-like variables and periodicity assumptions;
- horizontal versus inter-layer evolution conventions;
- `2π` versus `4π` periodic structure;
- coupled scalar/amplitude/phase descriptions;
- variational or action-based formulations;
- conservation laws and admissible constraints;
- observable projection rules;
- relation between local dynamics and domain-specific effective models.

The audit shall not promote an analogy, geometric picture, or historical discussion into a physical law without an explicit mathematical statement.

## Master Equation acceptance gate

A candidate master equation may enter computational testing only after all of the following are satisfied:

1. variables, domains, units, and parameters are explicit;
2. every independent assumption is labeled as a postulate;
3. every claimed consequence is either explicitly derived or clearly marked as conjectural;
4. dimensional consistency passes;
5. trivial, static, weak-field, or other relevant limiting cases are documented;
6. the equation has an unambiguous numerical interpretation;
7. at least one observable mapping is defined;
8. at least one failure condition is frozen before comparison with data;
9. parameter freedom is counted symmetrically against comparison models;
10. no v0.3 cosmological failure is erased or reclassified by the new formulation.

## Cross-domain sequence

The preferred development order is deliberately not another cosmology-only fitting campaign.

### Phase A — Fundamental structure

Produce the derivation/postulate/prediction ledger and one or more mathematically complete master-equation candidates.

Exit criterion: at least one candidate passes the Master Equation acceptance gate.

### Phase B — Galactic dynamics (RGMS candidate line)

Use galactic dynamics as the first independent specialization because it probes a different physical scale and observable family from cosmological expansion.

Initial targets should include rotation-curve observables with frozen preprocessing and comparison criteria. Established models and empirical baselines must receive symmetric parameter treatment.

Exit criterion: a preregistered result that can clearly favor, constrain, or falsify the candidate dynamics.

### Phase C — Lensing / gravitational consistency

Test whether the same dynamical structure that acts on galactic motion gives a compatible prediction for light deflection or another gravitational observable without introducing an unrelated rescue term.

Exit criterion: a cross-observable consistency or inconsistency statement.

### Phase D — Electromagnetic / laboratory-accessible sector

Derive a laboratory-scale prediction only if the mathematical mapping from the common dynamics is explicit. Prefer observables for which instrument/systematic errors can be independently bounded.

Exit criterion: a reproducible experimental protocol with an a priori null/positive/failure rule.

### Phase E — Quantum specialization (RQMS candidate line)

Only after the common state, phase, periodicity, and observable-projection rules are mathematically stable should the project attempt a quantum specialization.

The appearance of `4π`, phase, or spin-like behavior by itself shall not be treated as a quantum derivation.

Exit criterion: a distinct quantitative prediction or a rigorous correspondence result, not only qualitative analogy.

### Phase F — Return to cosmology

A revised RCMS line may be opened only after the common dynamics produces a cosmological correction by derivation or by a clearly identified new postulate.

Any new cosmological expression must be versioned separately from the frozen v0.3 logarithmic surrogate and retested from null calibration through held-out prediction.

## Anti-overfitting and anti-rescue rules

The following rules apply throughout v0.4:

- no parameter-domain widening after target inspection unless the original test is first recorded as failed/invalid;
- no replacement of a failed target by a more favorable target under the same protocol identifier;
- no hidden increase in free-parameter count;
- no use of descriptive recurrence as evidence of model selection;
- no promotion of curve-fit convenience terms to fundamental dynamics without derivation or explicit postulate status;
- unfavorable outcomes remain part of the permanent record;
- a new equation requires a new versioned hypothesis line, not retroactive modification of frozen results.

## Scientific success criteria for v0.4

v0.4 is scientifically successful even if the candidate dynamics fails, provided that the failure is discriminating and reproducible.

The strongest possible positive outcome would be a common dynamical structure that survives independent tests in at least two domains without domain-specific rescue terms. The minimum useful outcome is a precise identification of which postulate, derivation step, or observable mapping fails.
