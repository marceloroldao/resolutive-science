# v0.4 Fundamental Dynamics Roadmap

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — ISSUE #84

## Purpose

Reconstruct the smallest explicit mathematical chain for a candidate Resolutive Master Equation without using the v0.3/v0.4 cosmological residuals to tune the fundamental dynamics.

The cosmological RCMS surrogate remains historically frozen. This document starts a separate upstream reconstruction line.

## Classification vocabulary

Every statement must be labeled as one of:

- **D** — Derived;
- **P** — Postulated;
- **C** — Conjectural;
- **K** — Computational implementation;
- **O** — Observational input;
- **F** — Falsifiable prediction.

No analogy may be promoted to a physical law without an explicit mathematical step.

## Reconstruction chain

The reconstruction proceeds in this order and must not skip a layer:

1. primitive state variables;
2. state-space geometry;
3. periodicity/topology assumptions;
4. field content;
5. action or evolution law;
6. Euler-Lagrange/evolution equations;
7. conservation laws;
8. observable projection;
9. common parameter set;
10. domain-specific observables.

## Working candidate state

The following objects are admitted only as a candidate reconstruction ledger until individually classified:

- density-like scalar `rho > 0`;
- phase-like coordinate `Theta`;
- real scalar `chi`;
- optional orientation field `n in S^2`;
- complex representation `Psi = sqrt(rho) exp(i Theta/2)`.

At this stage the half-angle representation does **not** by itself establish physical `4pi` periodicity.

## Mandatory 4pi gate

Before any `4pi` rule can enter a Master Equation as physics, issue #85 must establish one of two outcomes:

1. **DERIVED_4PI** — the periodicity follows from the explicitly declared state space/topology and transformation law; or
2. **POSTULATED_4PI** — the periodicity is declared as an independent postulate and paired with at least one observable that distinguishes it from an otherwise equivalent `2pi` theory.

If neither condition is met, the fundamental dynamics must remain `2pi`-agnostic and the half-angle notation is treated as parametrization only.

## Candidate action family

A previously explored field representation motivates the following *candidate*, not yet accepted, action density:

```text
L_candidate = (partial rho)^2/(4 rho)
            + rho (partial Theta)^2/4
            + (partial chi)^2/2
            - V(rho, chi, n)
```

Classification at entry: **C/P pending derivation**.

Required checks before promotion:

- declare metric signature and contraction convention;
- specify dimensions/units of every field and coefficient;
- derive Euler-Lagrange equations explicitly;
- identify symmetries and Noether currents;
- derive at least one limiting case;
- show whether `n` is dynamical, constrained, auxiliary, or removable;
- show whether the candidate is equivalent to a conventional complex scalar field after field redefinition;
- isolate any genuinely new observable content.

## Anti-retuning rule

The following are forbidden during this reconstruction:

- choosing terms because they improve the already-seen RCMS cosmology residuals;
- inserting a redshift switch, survey coefficient, or dataset-specific amplitude;
- using the failed held-out BOSS result to choose a new functional form;
- treating a previously favorable SPARC/cosmology fit as derivation evidence.

Those results may later be used only as independent tests after the upstream equation is frozen.

## Phase A — Formal state-space audit

Deliverables:

- exact domain of `rho`, `Theta`, `chi`, and `n`;
- equivalence relation on `Theta`;
- transformation under `Theta -> Theta + 2pi` and `Theta -> Theta + 4pi`;
- whether `Psi` and `-Psi` denote the same physical state;
- topology of the total state space;
- dimensional table.

Gate: `STATE_SPACE_EXPLICIT`.

## Phase B — Action/evolution audit

Deliverables:

- frozen candidate action or an explicitly non-Lagrangian evolution law;
- equations of motion;
- well-posedness assumptions;
- conserved quantities;
- linearized spectrum around a simple equilibrium.

Gate: `DYNAMICS_EXPLICIT`.

## Phase C — Novelty/equivalence audit

The candidate must be compared against the nearest conventional formulations.

Questions:

- Is the half-angle representation merely a coordinate change?
- Does the theory reduce to a conventional scalar/phase field after redefinition?
- Does `4pi` create a measurable state distinction or only duplicate the covering space?
- Are extra layers physical degrees of freedom or bookkeeping labels?

Gate outcomes:

- `NONTRIVIAL_NEW_STRUCTURE`;
- `EQUIVALENT_REPARAMETRIZATION`;
- `UNDERDETERMINED`.

## Phase D — Observable map

Only after Phases A-C pass may the project derive domain observables.

The first observable map must be chosen before looking at target-domain fit results. Candidate domains include:

- controlled wave/interference system;
- coupled oscillator/layer-transition system;
- galactic dynamics under a preregistered RGMS protocol.

Cosmological extension is intentionally not first in line after the v0.4 diagnostic stop.

Gate: `OBSERVABLE_MAP_FROZEN`.

## Phase E — Cross-domain falsification

Issue #86 remains blocked until the upstream law and observable projection are frozen.

The cross-domain test must use symmetric baseline treatment, frozen nuisance handling, explicit boundary rules, and a genuine held-out target.
