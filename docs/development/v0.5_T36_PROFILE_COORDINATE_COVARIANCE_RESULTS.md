## 1. Executive result

The cumulative-ball and exact-shell profiles contain exactly the same finite-radius graph information, but the Euclidean profile-space diagnostics are not numerically invariant under this change of coordinates.

At the same time, the qualitative T35 conclusion recurs in both representations:

- overlap with the lower-scale subspace decreases as R increases;
- newly added higher-scale coordinates carry substantial power;
- the lower-scale subspace leaves a large higher-scale projection residual.

Therefore two statements must be separated:

1. **scale-dependent additional relational structure recurs across the two information-equivalent profiles;**
2. **the numerical Euclidean geometry assigned to that structure is coordinate dependent.**

No physical metric, tangent space, or spatial dimension is derived.

## 4. Coordinate dependence of the Euclidean profile metric

The B->S transformation is invertible but is not an orthogonal transformation of the Euclidean profile metric.

Accordingly:

- mean principal cosines move upward by roughly 0.08-0.10;
- outer-coordinate power shifts downward by roughly 0.17-0.23;
- projection residual shifts downward by roughly 0.10-0.19.

These are not measurement-noise effects: both representations are computed from exactly the same integer neighborhood counts.

Therefore:

```text
T36_EUCLIDEAN_PROFILE_GEOMETRY_COVARIANT = NOT_IDENTIFIED
T36_PROFILE_METRIC = COORDINATE_DEPENDENT
```

The T33-T35 Euclidean angle and norm values cannot be promoted to physical geometry unless a preferred metric or invariant construction is independently derived.

## 6. Size behavior

For each representation the diagnostics remain similar across N=128,256,512 for a fixed R->2R transition.

Therefore the coordinate sensitivity itself is reproducible across the frozen system-size matrix.

This rules out a simple finite-N explanation for the B/S discrepancy inside the tested ensemble.

## 7. Physical interpretation boundary

The correct interpretation is now narrower than after T35.

T35/T36 support:

> Finite-radius relational neighborhoods contain additional structure as the radius is enlarged, and this qualitative fact survives the cumulative/shell re-expression.

> The Euclidean vector geometry of either profile is a physical local geometry.

A physical geometric claim would require diagnostics invariant under admissible reparameterizations, or an independently justified internal metric that transforms covariantly with the coordinates.

The next admissible step is to abandon coordinate-dependent Euclidean angle as the primary candidate invariant and search for a **coordinate-free relational quantity** that is unchanged by the exact B<->S transformation.

