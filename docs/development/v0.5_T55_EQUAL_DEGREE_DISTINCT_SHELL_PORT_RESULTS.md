## 4. Exact limit of this result

Equal port resistance is only one macroscopic conventional control.

The three candidate nodes and their different branches may still differ in geometry, electric-field concentration, parasitic conductance/capacitance, temperature, heat transport, material inhomogeneity, and junction history. There is no basis to set their physical stochastic link-formation baseline hazards equal.

The run solves Kirchhoff equations for ideal resistors. It is **not** a laboratory observation.
