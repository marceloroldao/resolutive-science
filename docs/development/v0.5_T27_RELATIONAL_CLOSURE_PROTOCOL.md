# v0.5 T27 — Relational Closure Protocol

**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE DIMENSION ESTIMATION
**Parent control:** T25/T26 exact-tree generator remains unchanged.

## Question

Can local relational closure generate nontrivial cyclic geometry without coordinates, Euclidean distance, lattice information or a target dimension?

## Independent principle

**Relational closure:** if two Nódulos Resolutivos already participate in sufficiently overlapping local relational context, a direct Relação Resolutiva between them may become admissible.

This is motivated by local relational redundancy/closure, not by any desired dimensional exponent.

## Candidate D27-A

1. Generate the frozen T25 tree.
2. For every unordered non-edge pair (u,v), compute the number of common one-hop neighbors:
   c(u,v)=|N(u) intersect N(v)|.
3. A closure edge is admissible iff c(u,v) >= 1.
4. To prevent arbitrary ordering from selecting among admissible closures, compute the full admissible set from the pre-closure graph.
5. Add every admissible closure edge simultaneously.
6. Perform exactly one closure round.
7. No Euclidean coordinates, node-label distance, target degree, target dimension or post-result pruning is allowed.

The threshold c>=1 and one-round rule are frozen before execution.

## Why one round

Repeated closure can rapidly densify a graph and would introduce a new iteration-depth parameter. The first audit asks only what one minimal local closure operation does.

## Measurements

For N=128,256,512 and seeds 11,23,47:

- E/N and mean degree;
- beta_1=E-V+C and beta_1/N;
- triangle count and clustering;
- diameter/eccentricity;
- ball-growth local slopes and d_g;
- lazy spectral d_s(t);
- walk d_w;
- relabeling invariance;
- finite-size drift.

## Decision gates

## Forbidden interpretation

- cycles do not imply curvature automatically;
- triangles do not imply physical area;
- graph distance is not meters;
- closure is not gravity;
- any exponent near 3 is not selected as evidence of physical 3D without stability and operational mapping.
