## 1. Executive result

The non-tautological scale comparison does **not** identify a stable transported local subspace.

When the lower-scale row space is embedded by canonical zero-padding into the full higher-scale ambient space, three trends occur reproducibly:

1. principal-cosine overlap decreases as R increases;
2. a large and increasing fraction of higher-scale displacement power lies in the newly added coordinates;
3. projection residuals are large and increase with R.

Therefore the T34 apparent cross-scale stability was entirely removed once the higher-scale coordinates were retained.

The higher-radius profile contains genuine additional mathematical structure, but that structure does not behave as a scale-stable local tangent subspace under the frozen representation.

The next scientifically admissible question is no longer whether the T30 cumulative profile has a stable tangent subspace; T35 answers that negatively under canonical scale transport.

> Does the observed scale-dependent directional structure survive when the same underlying relational neighborhoods are encoded by materially different, preregistered coordinate-free profiles?
