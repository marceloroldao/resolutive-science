# v0.5 T4 — Continuum Bridge Audit

**Line:** v0.5 relational ontology — theory-only track  
**Depends on:** T1-T3  
**Experimental line:** E0/E1 FROZEN  
**Status:** THEORETICAL BRIDGE AUDIT  
**Target-data fitting:** NONE

## 1. Purpose

T1-T3 established a categorical/algebraic ontology, loop algebra and structural symmetry without assuming a continuum. T4 asks what additional structure is minimally required before derivatives, fields, local generators, variational actions and Noether-type reasoning become mathematically legitimate.

T4 does not assume that spacetime is fundamental or emergent. It audits four possible routes and marks every new assumption explicitly.

## 2. Starting point

The currently justified structure is

```text
C_R
```

with objects (Resolutive state classes), arrows (Resolutive transitions/trajectory classes), typed associative composition, identities, loop monoids/groups and categorical/discrete symmetries.

Not currently available:

```text
topology
metric
coordinates
real-valued distance
continuous path parameter
differentiable manifold
tangent space
connection
curvature
measure/integration
action functional
spacetime
```

## 3. Route A — Discrete fundamental ontology, continuous representation

Keep `C_R` fundamentally categorical/discrete and introduce a representation

```text
F : C_R -> C_cont
```

where `C_cont` contains continuous mathematical objects.

Examples of possible target structures include topological vector spaces, Hilbert-like spaces, manifolds, operator algebras or continuous state-space models. None is selected here.

Advantages:

- preserves the minimal ontology;
- makes continuity a property of representation rather than fundamental reality;
- allows multiple continuum realizations of the same relational structure.

Limitation:

- `F` is new physical/mathematical structure;
- continuity in `C_cont` does not prove continuity of `C_R`;
- different faithful/nonfaithful representations may yield different continuum descriptions.

Classification:

```text
T4_A = MINIMAL_CONTINUUM_REPRESENTATION_ROUTE
CONTINUUM_FUNDAMENTALITY = NOT_REQUIRED
```

## 4. Route B — Topologically enriched Resolutive category

Assign topology to morphism spaces (and, where appropriate, object structure), producing a topologically enriched category.

Schematically:

```text
Hom_R(A,B) = topological space
```

with continuous composition maps.

This permits continuous deformation concepts and may later support homotopy-like classifications.

New assumptions:

```text
topology on Hom spaces
continuity of composition
compatibility with physical quotient
```

Not obtained automatically:

```text
differentiability
metric
integration
action
```

Classification:

```text
T4_B = STRONGER_NEW_ASSUMPTION
```

## 5. Route C — Smooth/Lie groupoid-like sector

Restrict to a sector in which objects/arrows admit smooth manifold structure and source, target, identity, inversion (for the invertible sector) and composition maps satisfy appropriate smoothness conditions.

This yields a Lie-groupoid-like candidate when the full axioms and regularity conditions hold.

Potential gain:

- tangent/algebroid structures;
- infinitesimal generators;
- local continuous symmetry analysis.

New assumptions are substantial:

```text
manifold structure
smoothness
regularity
local finite-dimensionality or chosen generalization
smooth source/target/composition
```

Classification:

```text
T4_C = POWERFUL_BUT_NOT_DERIVED
```

## 6. Route D — Direct field postulate on a manifold

Postulate a manifold `M` and fields

```text
Phi : M -> V
```

then construct a Lagrangian/action.

This is the conventional shortest route to field theory, but it bypasses the question of how the continuum relates to the categorical Resolutive ontology.

Classification:

```text
T4_D = VALID_MODELING_POSTULATE
T4_D = NOT_DERIVED_FROM_C_R
T4_D = NOT_SELECTED_AS_FOUNDATIONAL_ROUTE
```

## 7. Selection by primitive economy

Under the frozen P0 criterion of primitive economy, Route A introduces less fundamental structure than B-D because it leaves `C_R` unchanged and places continuity in a representation layer.

Therefore T4 adopts Route A only as the **minimal working bridge**, not as a claim that nature is fundamentally discrete.

```text
T4_WORKING_BRIDGE = ROUTE_A
```

This is a methodological selection. It is not empirical evidence for discreteness.

## 8. Resolutive continuum representation

Define conditionally

```text
F_c : C_R -> C_cont.
```

A continuum representation must preserve at least identity and composition:

```text
F_c(I_A) = I_{F_c(A)}
F_c(Gamma_2 o Gamma_1)
 = F_c(Gamma_2) o F_c(Gamma_1).
```

It must also respect physical equivalence/quotients.

If `F_c` is nonfaithful, distinct Resolutive trajectories may collapse to the same continuum representation.

If faithful on a chosen sector, algebraically distinct arrows remain distinct there.

Thus representation faithfulness becomes an explicit modeling property.

## 9. Continuity is not yet differentiability

Even after choosing `C_cont`, continuity alone does not justify derivatives.

To define a derivative, a selected represented sector must carry additional local linear/smooth structure.

Therefore:

```text
CONTINUUM_REPRESENTATION ->/-> DERIVATIVE
```

A differentiable bridge requires a second gate.

## 10. Minimal parameterized family

Suppose a represented trajectory admits a continuous parameter `lambda`:

```text
x(lambda).
```

At this stage `lambda` is only an ordering/parameter coordinate of the representation.

It must not be called physical time.

Reparameterizations

```text
lambda -> f(lambda)
```

may describe the same underlying ordered trajectory if the future theory declares parameterization physically redundant.

This introduces a major fork:

```text
PARAMETER_IS_PHYSICAL
vs
PARAMETER_IS_GAUGE/BOOKKEEPING.
```

Neither is selected in T4.

## 11. A first continuum consistency requirement

If a continuous representation is merely a representation of the same Resolutive composition, subdivision of a represented path must agree with categorical composition.

For `lambda_0 < lambda_1 < lambda_2`, schematically:

```text
F_c(Gamma[lambda_0,lambda_2])
=
F_c(Gamma[lambda_1,lambda_2]) o F_c(Gamma[lambda_0,lambda_1]).
```

This is the continuum analogue of P1 segmentation consistency.

It is inherited structurally, not fitted.

## 12. Where topology can first enter without being fundamental

The target `C_cont` may carry topology even when `C_R` does not.

This permits notions such as convergence of represented states/trajectories and continuous families of represented transformations.

But topological invariants then belong initially to the representation. They cannot automatically be promoted to invariants of the fundamental Resolutive ontology unless shown representation-independent.

## 13. Where 2pi/4pi could legally reappear

T4 identifies, for the first time, a legitimate future route for angular periodicity:

1. a continuum representation is selected;
2. its symmetry/representation space contains a circle, rotation group, covering group or related topology;
3. the physical representation selects how states transform;
4. periodicity follows from that independently justified representation.

Therefore 2pi or 4pi may later emerge from representation/topology, but neither is present in T4 itself.

```text
T4_2PI = NOT_DERIVED
T4_4PI = NOT_DERIVED
```

## 14. Action integrals are still illegal

An expression such as

```text
S = integral L(Phi, partial Phi, ...) dmu
```

requires at minimum:

- a domain with measure/integration structure;
- fields/configuration variables;
- differentiability if derivatives occur;
- a Lagrangian object;
- physical interpretation of variations/boundaries.

Route A alone supplies none of these uniquely.

Thus:

```text
T4_ACTION = NOT_DERIVED
```
