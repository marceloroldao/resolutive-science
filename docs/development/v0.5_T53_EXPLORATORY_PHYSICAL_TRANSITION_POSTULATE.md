# v0.5 T53 — Minimal Dimensioned Transition Postulate (Exploratory, Not Derived)

**Status:** CANDIDATE POSTULATE ONLY; NOT DERIVED FROM RSMS OR T49; NO PHYSICAL VALIDATION  
**Parent:** T52. **Scope:** make the first possible physical question falsifiable without rebranding a static resistor network.

## 1. Why transition events, not static terminal resistance?

T52 proves that an added static local reciprocal linear edge coefficient is ordinary weighted-network conductance after measurement. Static terminal resistance cannot uniquely identify a new interaction.

The T25–T49 mechanism is about **which new connection forms** under competition, rather than the terminal response of an already fixed graph.

A possible operational bridge is therefore a system containing real stochastic candidate junctions whose connection events occur physically, without a controller implementing the T25 algorithm.

This is a proposal for an experiment class, not evidence of a new force.

## 2. Objective-node condition

A node is a separately fabricated, independently identifiable physical contact/junction with an operational existence criterion. Each present edge is an actual physical conductive connection.

A mere schematic point inserted by dividing a resistor in series does **not** create an additional resolutive node. If node identity cannot be fixed independently of the desired graph statistic, the physical bridge fails the T52 refinement gate.

For every candidate physical junction, the graph has to specify which actually formed connections are counted and how leakage/partial formation is treated **before** events are observed.

## 3. A structural variable distinct from degree

Let, for a connected physical contact graph,

d(u) = number of formed contacts incident to u,

L2(u) = number of distinct physical contacts at exact graph distance two from u,

q(u)=L2(u)/d(u)  for d(u)>0.

q is dimensionless. The candidate comparison is restricted to d(u)=d(w)>0 with q(u) != q(w), so the proposed quantity is statistically distinguishable from the current T25 degree rule.

No assertion is made that q represents physical energy or density.

## 4. Independently calibrated conventional baseline

Let k_u^0 >0 and k_w^0 >0 be the predicted or independently calibrated **physical event hazards**, in s^-1, under specified junction conditions.

The comparator must account for the known local material and field variables relevant to the chosen physical implementation, including measured local voltage, temperature, contact geometry, activation history and environment.

Do not assume k_u^0=k_w^0 merely because graph degrees are equal.

Stochastic conductive-filament systems in particular have known contributions from ionic migration, redox, local electric fields, Joule heating, material disorder and history. Their omission does not constitute new physics.

## 5. Explicit exploratory Resolutive ansatz

For competing candidate contacts u and w define

log[k_u/k_w] =
  log[k_u^0/k_w^0] - lambda_R [q(u)-q(w)].

Here:

- k_u,k_w and k_u^0,k_w^0 have units s^-1;
- q and lambda_R are dimensionless;
- lambda_R>0 is a **new, independent phenomenological hypothesis**, not a result derived from T49;
- lambda_R=0 is the conventional-baseline null.

Equivalently, one may write a dimensioned effective activation-barrier offset

Delta F_u^R = k_B T lambda_R q(u)   [J],

but that is a parametrization of the event-rate ansatz, **not an established microscopic energy or new thermodynamic law**.

The model must be rejected rather than re-sign lambda_R if the preregistered positive-direction contrast is contradicted.

## 6. Discriminating conditional prediction

Define the baseline-adjusted log-odds contrast

D(u,w)=log[(k_u/k_w)/(k_u^0/k_w^0)].

The ansatz predicts

D(u,w)=-lambda_R Delta q,

where Delta q=q(u)-q(w).

For two held-out conditions in the same material/temperature domain with Delta q=1 and 2:

D_2=2 D_1 <0,

provided lambda_R>0.

For two competing memoryless hazards, first-event odds equal hazard ratios, so the same contrast may be measured from first-event frequencies if the exponential-hazard and censoring assumptions pass their own diagnostic gates.

A separately estimated lambda_R from a disjoint calibration cohort may predict both held-out contrasts; no fit to the confirmation cohort is allowed.

**No absolute magnitude of lambda_R has been derived from v0.5.** Consequently the unconditional, parameter-free new-physics prediction is not yet available.

## 7. Against trivial imitation

If a digital controller or programmable switch chooses u with probability proportional to exp(-lambda_R q(u)), observing this response verifies only the implementation of the algorithm, not spontaneous physical selection.

A physical test requires natural events from actual candidate junctions, with no circuit/software rule receiving q and deciding which junction forms.

An apparent nonzero lambda_R may still represent incomplete conventional modeling of geometry, field, temperature or materials. Replication of this phenomenological form alone is insufficient to identify a fundamental interaction.

## 8. Representation and physical-invariance checks

The same device must not change q merely by drawing an extra node in its schematic.

A physical rewiring that genuinely changes the realized network is a change of experimental system and requires new conventional predictions.

At otherwise identical local candidate conditions, remote rewiring producing a known Delta q permits a nontrivial within-device crossover, but ordinary field and thermal coupling caused by rewiring must be measured and included in k^0.

## 9. Explicit failure conditions

The candidate is not promoted when any of the following occurs:

- the objective-node / refinement gate fails;
- equal-degree, distinct-q candidate comparisons cannot be physically realized;
- the calibrated physical comparator is inaccurate on negative controls;
- lambda_R fitted on the calibration cohort does not transfer to blinded held-out structures;
- the sign is opposite the preregistered lambda_R>0 direction;
- the measured effect is explainable by measured known conventional variables;
- nonexponential hazards invalidate the chosen odds estimator without a frozen alternative;
- the effect occurs only when software explicitly implements the rule.

## 11. Decision

T53 is deliberately a **testable phenomenological bridge candidate**, not a frozen Equação Mestra or confirmation of a new physical law.

A confirmatory experiment may test it only after the objective-node criterion and independently calibrated standard physical comparator are specified. No fitting of lambda_R to the eventual held-out observation is permitted.
