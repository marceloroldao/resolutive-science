## 1. Executive result

No single preregistered topology diagnostic H1..H8 satisfies the full T39 stable-source gate.

Therefore:

```text
T39_STABLE_SOURCE_CANDIDATE = NONE_IDENTIFIED
```

However the audit reveals a reproducible two-part structure:

1. the 9 T25 parent trees are systematically more globally compact than their degree-matched nulls;
2. within the null ensembles, inequality/concentration of node betweenness (H8) is the strongest and most stable correlate of the joint T38 high-radius departure.

Thus the T38 specificity is not reduced to one scalar source under the frozen criteria. The next hypothesis should treat the source as a structured combination rather than silently choosing the best single correlate.

## 3. Global compactness family

Three metrics show the same parent-null departure in all 9 cases:

```text
H1 diameter                 = LOW 9/9
H2 mean pairwise distance   = LOW 9/9
H3 mean eccentricity        = LOW 9/9
```

Observed parent ranges versus null-ensemble mean ranges:

```text
H1 parent diameter: 25 .. 42
H1 null means:       40.99 .. 96.20

H2 parent mean distance: 12.27 .. 18.95
H2 null means:             16.07 .. 36.79

H3 parent mean eccentricity: 20.60 .. 31.94
H3 null means:               31.37 .. 72.64
```

So the T25 construction produces trees that are substantially more compact than random labeled trees with the same degree sequence.

The compactness metrics also correlate with the joint M3/M7 departure in the expected negative direction in all 9 null ensembles, but not strongly enough to pass the frozen |rho|>=0.50 median criterion:

```text
median |rho(H1,Z37)| = 0.377
median |rho(H2,Z37)| = 0.463
median |rho(H3,Z37)| = 0.368
```

Therefore compactness is a stable structural departure but not a sufficient single-variable source under T39.

## 4. Degree assortativity

H4 is higher than the degree-matched null range in 8 of 9 parents and inside the interval in 1 of 9.

Its correlation signs with M3, M7 and Z37 are positive in all 9 null ensembles.

```text
median |rho(H4,Z37)| = 0.425
```

This does not pass the frozen parent-extremity or effect-size gates, but indicates that how equal/unequal degree vertices are arranged along edges contributes to the high-radius structure.

## 5. Betweenness concentration

H8, the Gini concentration of normalized node betweenness, is the strongest preregistered correlate of the joint departure.

Across all 9 null ensembles:

```text
rho(H8,Z37) > 0
rho(H8,M3)  > 0
rho(H8,M7)  > 0
```

with

```text
median |rho(H8,Z37)| = 0.766
range rho(H8,Z37) = 0.583 .. 0.828

median rho(H8,M3) = 0.695
median rho(H8,M7) = 0.689
```

But the T25 parent H8 value is not outside the central 95% null interval in the same direction for all 9 parents.

Therefore:

```text
H8 = STRONG_STABLE_CORRELATE
H8 = NOT_STABLE_SINGLE_SOURCE
```

T39 does not promote H8 as the unique source.

## 6. Branch-length and maximum-centrality candidates

H5 mean branch length:      no useful null variation

These candidates are not carried forward as primary source variables.

## 7. Structural interpretation

The current evidence supports a distributed structural picture:

```text
T25 higher-order organization
~ global compactness
+ degree-placement organization
+ concentration pattern of path mediation
```

This expression is descriptive, not a derived equation.

The data do not justify replacing that structure by a single scalar invariant.

In particular, H8 explains variation of M3/M7 within degree-matched tree ensembles better than the other preregistered single metrics, while H1-H3 identify the most universal parent-null departure.

That distinction is important:

- **extremity** asks what makes the T25 parents unusual;
- **correlation** asks what tracks M3/M7 variation inside the null family.

The same variable need not optimize both.

## 9. Consequence

The next admissible test is a preregistered **conditional structural audit**, not another single-metric search.

A clean T40 should test whether the stable M3/M7 departure remains after simultaneously controlling for:

1. a fixed compactness composite built from H1-H3;
2. H4 degree assortativity;
3. H8 betweenness Gini.

The variables must be frozen before execution and no feature selection may occur.

If these controls remove the parent M3/M7 departure, the T38 specificity can be classified as statistically accounted for by this higher-order topology family.

If a stable residual remains, an additional unmeasured structural invariant is required.

No physical dynamics should be introduced before that conditional audit.
