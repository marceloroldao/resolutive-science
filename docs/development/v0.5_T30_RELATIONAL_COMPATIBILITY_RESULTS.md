# v0.5 T30 — Relational Compatibility Results

**Protocol:** frozen T30.
**Parent:** frozen T25 graphs.
**Matrix:** N=128,256,512; seeds 11,23,47; R=1,2,4,8,16.
**No threshold. No edge dynamics.**

## Test

For each preregistered condition, evaluate the RMS cumulative shell-mass distance

D_R(u,v) = sqrt[(1/(R+1)) sum_r (m_r(u)-m_r(v))^2]

for nonadjacent pairs in R < d_G(u,v) <= 2R, and C_R=1/(1+D_R).

## Structural observations

D_R is nonnegative, relabeling invariant and bounded because every cumulative mass m_r lies in [0,1].

The observable is not equivalent to the exact T28 signature: many distinct shell-degree structures can share nearby cumulative shell-mass profiles.

## Empirical pattern

Across the frozen matrix, the band distributions remain nonzero and possess finite spread at R=1,2,4,8. Unlike exact signature equality, the continuous observable does not disappear at R=8.

At R=16 finite-size saturation becomes material, consistent with T29: cumulative masses approach one as radius covers a large fraction of the graph, compressing profile differences.

The principal qualitative result is therefore:

T30_CONTINUOUS_COMPATIBILITY_SURVIVES_R8 = YES

while

T30_EXACT_MATCH_CONTROL_AT_R8 = EXTINCT_IN_T29.

## Interpretation

T29's termination was not caused by absence of relational variation. It was caused by representing compatibility as an exact equivalence relation on an increasingly detailed discrete signature.

T30 shows that relational differences can instead remain measurable continuously at the same scale where exact equality vanishes.

This is a representation result, not yet a dynamical law.

## Scale-stability gate

The observable is nondegenerate over the pre-saturation scales, but a universal scale-invariant distribution has not been established. The finite graphs show radius- and size-dependent drift, especially as R approaches graph eccentricity scales.

Therefore:

T30_COMPATIBILITY_OBSERVABLE = NONDEGENERATE
T30_SCALE_STABILITY = NOT_YET_IDENTIFIED
T30_UNIVERSAL_COMPATIBILITY_DISTRIBUTION = NOT_DERIVED

## Critical restriction

No threshold is inferred from the distributions. In particular, no percentile, mean, median, fixed C_R value or nearest-neighbor rule is promoted post hoc into D30 dynamics.

Doing so would convert a diagnostic success into an unfrozen fitted transition rule.

## Consequence for T31

The next question is not which threshold to choose.

T31 should ask whether a transition law can be derived from **relative compatibility flow** rather than absolute compatibility: e.g. whether changes/gradients of D_R along existing relations provide an intrinsic direction or extremal principle without a fitted cutoff.

Such a law must be preregistered independently before any graph evolution is executed.
