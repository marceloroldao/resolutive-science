## 5. Interpretation

T72 showed that the missing T70/T71 directions are locally reachable.

T73 now shows that the **transition kernel itself has a nonzero local drift in the same direction as the observed post-burn-in one-sided support**:

- toward smaller A1;
- toward less-negative C_KR.

This supports an entropic/transition-measure explanation for the T70/T71 bracketing failure rather than a local geometric boundary.

T73 does not derive the full stationary distribution of the constrained chain.
