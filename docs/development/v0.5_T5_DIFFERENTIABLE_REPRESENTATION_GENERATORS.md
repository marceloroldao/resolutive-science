## 6. Ordered exponential

The formal solution is the path/parameter-ordered exponential

```text

=
P exp[
  integral_(lambda_1)^(lambda_2) G_R(lambda) d_lambda

```

Ordering is required in general because

```text

```

need not vanish.

If all generators commute along the path, ordering becomes unnecessary and the expression reduces to an ordinary exponential of the integral.




This suggests that the more primitive represented object is not `G_R` alone but


This does not establish physical reversibility or an inverse transition for every trajectory. It only states how a reversible represented parameterization behaves when an inverse segment exists.

