# v0.5 T51 — Dimensioned Physical Load and Operational No-Go

**Status:** ANALYTICAL RESULT; EXPERIMENTAL NOVELTY GATE NOT SATISFIED  
**Parent:** T50. **Purpose:** distinguish graph structure from a genuine physical state.

## 1. Exact question

Can a pure unweighted graph statistic, such as degree d_i or two-shell occupancy |S_2(i)|, be identified universally with dissipated electrical power?

The answer is no, independently of the T25 generator.

## 2. Conventional physical realization

Take a connected undirected graph G whose edges are ordinary linear resistors. Each edge e={i,j} has conductance g_e > 0 in siemens.

Let V_i be the measured node potential in volts. Edge current from i to j is

I_ij = g_ij (V_i-V_j)  [A].

The conductance-weighted Laplacian L_g is determined by

(L_g)ii=sum_j g_ij,
(L_g)ij=-g_ij for i!=j.

The physical balance relation is

L_g V = I_ext.

The gauge freedom V->V+c*1 leaves all edge voltage differences and currents unchanged. For net-zero current injection and connected G, solutions are unique up to that gauge.

Total dissipated power:

P_total=sum_{edges {i,j}} g_ij(V_i-V_j)^2
       = V^T L_g V   [W].

A symmetric bookkeeping allocation to nodes is

P_i=(1/2)sum_{j adjacent i}g_ij(V_i-V_j)^2  [W],

so sum_i P_i=P_total.

This is a conventional model and not a Resolutive law.

## 3. Scaling no-go

With fixed G and fixed conductances, multiply every externally applied potential difference by a factor s.

Then

V'_i-V'_j = s(V_i-V_j);
I'_ij = s I_ij;
P'_i = s^2 P_i;
P'_total=s^2 P_total.

However for every node i,

d'_i=d_i,
|S'_2(i)|=|S_2(i)|,
W(G')=W(G).

Therefore a graph-only scalar F_i(G) cannot equal physical dissipated power P_i for **all** nonzero source amplitudes.

An explicit counterexample is s=2: identical graph statistics and fourfold power.

Conclusion:

T51_GRAPH_ONLY_POWER_IDENTIFICATION = MATHEMATICALLY_IMPOSSIBLE_AS_UNIVERSAL_RELATION.

The same argument prevents direct universal identification with any drive-dependent state variable unless the excitation/physical state is supplied independently.

## 4. Local physical load is not degree

The physically measurable nodal power is

P_i=(1/2)sum_{j adjacent i} g_ij (Delta V_ij)^2.

Even two nodes with the same d_i can have different P_i due to different edge conductances or voltage differences.

Consequently, a putative Resolutive 'local load' must clearly say whether it means:

(a) a topological count,
(b) an operational physical observable,
(c) a dynamical internal field with separately stated units and equations.

A pure rename from (a) to (b) or (c) is disallowed.

## 5. Conventional terminal observable

For current injected at terminal a and extracted at b, let b_ab=e_a-e_b and apply I_ext=I*b_ab.

The ordinary predicted effective resistance is

R_eff(a,b)=b_ab^T L_g^+ b_ab    [ohm].

A change of R_eff between graph topologies is already predicted by conventional circuit theory. Such a change is not, by itself, evidence of novel physics.

## 6. Experimental calibration, not novelty claim

One could construct two low-voltage resistor networks with:

- the same number of nodes and edges;
- the same multiset or labeled degree sequence;
- different two-shell topology;
- independently measured edge resistances.

A conventional baseline must predict R_eff for each network **before** measurement.

Record instrument accuracy, resistor tolerance, lead/contact resistance, temperature, nonlinearity and the applied drive.

This calibrates whether our proposed graph-to-observable map describes a physical realization. It does not test a new physical law until a separately derived alternative correction has a preregistered magnitude/sign and failure criterion.

## 7. Novel-physics stop criterion

Do not count any of the following as evidence of a new interaction:

- the graph generator's T49 dose response;
- a difference between resistor topologies already predicted by L_g;
- an unmodeled laboratory residual;
- a post-hoc adjustment that tracks the residual.

A genuinely discriminating prediction requires an independently motivated Resolutive dynamical/constitutive relation F_R and an observable O such that

O_R(parameters fixed independently) != O_standard

in a specified experimental domain.

Before observation, record the direction, uncertainty band, matched conventional comparator, and conditions that falsify F_R.
