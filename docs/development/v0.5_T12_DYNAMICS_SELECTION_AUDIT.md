# v0.5 T12 — Dynamics Selection Audit

**Line:** v0.5 relational ontology — theory-only track
**Depends on:** T1-T11
**Experimental line:** E0/E1 FROZEN
**Status:** DYNAMICS ROUTE SELECTION
**Target-data fitting:** NONE

## 1. Purpose

T1-T11 constructed a relational/categorical ontology, ordered trajectories, continuous representations, local generators, connection, curvature, topology/holonomy, operational relational clocks and a conditional route to energy. No unique dynamics has yet been derived.

T12 compares candidate dynamical routes by primitive economy and falsifiability. It does not choose a familiar field theory merely because such a theory is mathematically available.

## 2. Selection criteria

A candidate route is assessed against:

```text
C1 — primitive economy: add as little new fundamental structure as possible;
C2 — ontology compatibility: preserve composition/history structure from T1-T11;
C3 — reparameterization discipline: do not turn arbitrary lambda into absolute time;
C4 — predictive closure: produce actual admissibility/evolution equations;
C5 — falsifiability: permit predictions that can fail;
C6 — representation neutrality: avoid prematurely imposing spacetime metric, quantum Hilbert space or legacy RSMS variables;
C7 — continuum independence: retain a path back to the fundamental categorical/discrete level.
```

## 3. Route R1 — Discrete/categorical update law

Postulate a local admissibility/update rule directly on Resolutive states/arrows:

```text
Gamma_{k+1} in D(Gamma_k, local relational data).
```

or deterministically

```text
X_{k+1} = Phi_R(X_k).
```

Advantages:

- closest to the primitive categorical ontology;
- requires no metric, measure or continuum;
- directly falsifiable once observables/readout maps are defined;
- continuum dynamics may later arise as a representation/coarse-graining.

Costs:

- the rule `Phi_R` or admissibility relation is new dynamical input;
- conservation/symmetry properties are not automatic;
- continuum covariance must be recovered rather than assumed.

Classification:

```text
R1 = MINIMUM FOUNDATIONAL DYNAMICS ROUTE
```

## 4. Route R2 — Reparameterization-invariant relational variational action

Introduce configuration variables `q^A(lambda)` and an action invariant under monotonic reparameterizations:

```text
S_R[q] = integral L_R(q, dq/dlambda, ...) dlambda.
```

A homogeneous degree-one Lagrangian or equivalent constrained construction can make the action insensitive to the arbitrary trajectory parameter.

Advantages:

- yields Euler-Lagrange equations;
- naturally connects symmetry to conserved quantities;
- compatible with T10 relational time after choosing an internal clock.

Costs:

- action principle is a new postulate;
- scalar Lagrangian is not selected by T1-T11;
- integration/regularity/configuration structure must be added;
- many inequivalent Lagrangians satisfy the same symmetry requirements.

Classification:

```text
R2 = STRONG CONTINUUM DYNAMICS ROUTE
R2 = NOT MINIMAL FOUNDATIONAL START
```

## 5. Route R3 — Hamiltonian/symplectic dynamics

Introduce a phase space, symplectic/Poisson structure and constraint/Hamiltonian generating relational evolution.

Advantages:

- precise generator/observable structure;
- clean route toward energy and canonical quantization if later justified.

Costs:

- substantially more primitives than R1;
- no symplectic form has been derived;
- canonical coordinates/momenta are not yet ontological primitives.

Classification:

```text
R3 = DEFERRED
```

## 6. Route R4 — Metric-free topological dynamics

Use T8 structures such as flatness, BF-like or Chern-Simons-like actions.

Advantages:

- no metric required;
- naturally compatible with connection/holonomy.

Costs:

- dimension, orientation, invariant pairing and/or auxiliary fields must be selected;
- many such theories are topological and do not by themselves provide the local propagating phenomenology eventually required;
- nothing currently selects one topological action.

Classification:

```text
R4 = AVAILABLE SPECIAL SECTOR
R4 = NOT UNIVERSAL DYNAMICS
```

## 7. Route R5 — Metric-dependent field dynamics

Add a base metric, measure and field action, allowing terms such as curvature norms and scalar kinetic terms.

Advantages:

- direct contact with conventional relativistic field theory and empirical models.

Costs:

- T7 found no derived metric or signature;
- introduces the largest amount of unselected structure;
- risks reverse-engineering known physics into the ontology.

Classification:

```text
R5 = PHENOMENOLOGICAL/EMERGENT ROUTE ONLY AT PRESENT
```

## 8. Selection

By C1-C7, T12 selects **R1: discrete/categorical update/admissibility dynamics** as the foundational dynamics route.

This does not mean nature is proven discrete. It means the new formulation should place its first dynamical postulate at the same structural level as its most economical ontology, then derive/test continuum representations separately.

Frozen decision:

```text
T12_FOUNDATIONAL_DYNAMICS_ROUTE = R1
```

## 9. Minimal dynamical primitive

Introduce exactly one new primitive class:

> **Resolutive Transition Law** `D_R` — a local rule that assigns to an admissible Resolutive state/context the set or distribution of allowed successor transitions.

Abstractly:

```text
D_R : local relational context -> admissible next arrows/states.
```

Deterministic special case:

```text
X_{k+1} = Phi_R(X_k, N_k)
```

where `N_k` denotes explicitly defined relational neighborhood/context data.

Stochastic special case:

```text
P_R(X_{k+1} | X_k, N_k).
```

T12 does not choose deterministic versus stochastic dynamics.

## 10. Locality must be relational, not spatially assumed

Because physical space has not been derived, `local` cannot mean 'nearby in meters'.

Define **Resolutive Locality** provisionally as dependence only on a bounded/declared relational neighborhood in the primitive interaction/composition structure.

Thus:

```text
RELATIONAL_LOCALITY != METRIC_SPATIAL_LOCALITY
```

until a bridge between them is derived.

## 11. Composition consistency constraint

The transition law must respect the categorical typing and composition rules:

```text
source(Gamma_{k+1}) = target(Gamma_k)
```

for sequential evolution, and physically equivalent descriptions must remain equivalent under the update.

This is not optional phenomenology; it is required for consistency with T1.

## 12. Symmetry covariance constraint

For every symmetry `S` declared physical in the relevant sector, dynamics must satisfy a covariance condition schematically

```text
D_R(S X) = S D_R(X)
```

with the appropriate set/distribution interpretation.

This prevents the dynamics from depending on arbitrary labels or representation choices.

## 13. History sensitivity

T2-T9 showed that endpoint equality need not erase trajectory information. Therefore the dynamical state supplied to `D_R` may need more than an endpoint object.

A minimal state can be represented abstractly as

```text
Sigma_k = (X_k, H_k)
```

where `H_k` is only the physically relevant history quotient/invariant, not the entire raw past.

The law becomes

```text
D_R(Sigma_k) -> Sigma_{k+1}.
```

Whether `H_k` is necessary is experimentally/model dependent. T12 does not force non-Markovianity, but it explicitly permits it without storing an arbitrary full trajectory.

## 14. Candidate compressed history variables

T1-T11 provide disciplined candidates for `H_k` depending on representation:

```text
loop/word class
commutator class
holonomy conjugacy class
kernel/image quotient label
topological sector
```

No candidate is universally selected yet.

This gives a concrete path to test the old Resolutive idea that history can affect present behavior.

## 15. Continuum recovery requirement

A valid foundational `D_R` must be tested for a regime in which coarse-grained/represented evolution approximates

```text
dU/dlambda = G_R U
```

and, in multidirectional representations, connection/curvature structures from T5-T6.

Thus continuum equations become **derived effective limits** of the selected update law where possible, rather than foundational assumptions.

## 16. Falsifiability requirements

No proposed `D_R` qualifies as a physical law until it specifies:

1. state variables and admissible contexts;
2. exact update/admissibility rule;
3. free parameters and dimensions;
4. observable/readout map;
5. initial/boundary conditions;
6. at least one quantitative prediction differing from a baseline theory or null model;
7. a preregistered failure criterion.

Without these, `D_R` is only a mathematical model family.

## 17. Status of a Master Equation

T12 does **not** yet authorize a unique numerical Master Equation because the functional form of `D_R` has not been selected by derivation or data.

What T12 does authorize is a **Master Dynamical Schema**:

```text
Sigma_{k+1} in D_R(Sigma_k; theta_R)
```

subject to:

```text
categorical consistency
symmetry covariance
relational locality
explicit history quotient if used
observable map
falsifiability conditions.
```

This is the narrowest honest dynamical statement currently justified.

## 19. Main theoretical consequence

The new Resolutive formulation should not begin with a continuum Master Action. Its minimal predictive core should begin with a **Resolutive Transition Law** on relational states/history classes.

The continuum structures developed in T4-T11 then become consistency targets and emergent representations of that law.

Conceptually:

```text
primitive relational state
        |
        v
Resolutive Transition Law D_R
        |
        +--> ordered history / loops
        +--> coarse-grained generator
        +--> connection / curvature
        +--> topology / holonomy
        +--> operational relational time
        +--> conditional energy structure
```
