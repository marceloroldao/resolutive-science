### 2. Neutral edges

Neutral-edge fraction falls strongly as R increases.

Across seeds:
- N=128: about 0.33--0.46 at R=1 -> 0.00037--0.00086 at R=8.
- N=256: about 0.45--0.46 -> 0.00043--0.00067.
- N=512: about 0.43--0.47 -> 0.00052--0.00078.

Thus the field becomes much less locally degenerate at larger R.

### 3. Greedy descent reachability

Despite the nonzero gradient, greedy local descent almost never reaches the chosen reference.

Mean reach fractions across references:

- N=128: ~1.4--1.6% at R=1; ~7.1--10.1% at R=8.
- N=256: ~0.72--0.74% at R=1; ~3.9--5.3% at R=8.
- N=512: ~0.36% at R=1; ~2.3--2.7% at R=8.

Reachability decreases strongly with increasing N.

### 4. Traps dominate

Mean trap fractions are roughly:
- N=128: 0.86--0.91 depending on seed/R;
- N=256: 0.89--0.94;
- N=512: 0.89--0.95.

At R=8 specifically, traps remain ~0.87--0.90 (N=128), ~0.90--0.93 (N=256), and ~0.94--0.95 (N=512).

Ambiguity decreases with R, so the failure of descent is not primarily caused by ties. It is dominated by local minima/non-monotone landscape structure.

## Important scaling observation

The raw G magnitude decreases strongly with N at fixed R because the T30 profile uses cumulative shell mass normalized by total N. Therefore G>0 is robust as a signal of variation, but raw G is not an N-independent physical scale.
