## 3. Minimality criteria

A quantity qualifies as a universal physical primitive only if all of the following are available independently of target-data fitting:

1. mathematical domain and equivalence structure;
2. physical or operational meaning;
3. dimensions and units, or an explicit justified dimensionless status;
4. transformation law;
5. reason it cannot be computed from a smaller primitive set;
6. role in dynamics or constraints;
7. observable projection or a preregisterable discriminator.

Failure of items 2–7 does not make a quantity mathematically invalid. It means the quantity is not yet justified as a **universal physical primitive**.
