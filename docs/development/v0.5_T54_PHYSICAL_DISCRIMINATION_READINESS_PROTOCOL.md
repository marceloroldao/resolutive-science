# v0.5 T54 — Physical Discrimination Readiness and Low-Voltage Calibration Protocol

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN; NOT EXECUTED; CONFIRMATORY SAMPLE SIZE NOT YET FROZEN  
**Parents:** T51–T53. **Scope:** distinguish ordinary circuit topology, programmed behavior and genuinely spontaneous event statistics.

## 1. Two explicitly separate experimental tiers

**Tier 0:** conventional low-voltage resistor-network calibration. Purpose: verify topology-to-observable calculations, gauge-invariant measurements and representation/refinement invariance. Passing is **not evidence of new physics**.

**Tier 1:** independently realized stochastic physical link formation with measured event hazards. Purpose: test the T53 exploratory postulate. Hardware whose control software implements degree/q choice **cannot** serve as a physical discovery test.

No Tier-1 confirmation is allowed before the null comparator, physically objective nodes, parameter calibration, thresholds and sample size are frozen.

## 2. Tier 0: exact refinement control

Build two alternative purely passive circuits.

**A:**
a --(2.0 kOhm)-- b --(1.0 kOhm)-- c.

**B:**
a --(1.0 kOhm)-- x --(1.0 kOhm)-- b --(1.0 kOhm)-- c.

The x node of B has no shunt, load or connection other than the two series resistors. Thus the terminal resistance between a and c is nominally 3.0 kOhm in both circuits.

At 3.0 V DC ideal predictions are:
- I=1.0 mA;
- P_total=3.0 mW.

At 6.0 V DC ideal predictions are:
- I=2.0 mA;
- P_total=12.0 mW.

Both graph representations have different node counts and wedge counts:
- A: W=1, |S2| total=2.
- B: W=2, |S2| total=4.

Nevertheless the electrical port response is the same within independently propagated component and meter uncertainties.

Record measured resistor values (rather than assuming their nominal values), DMM current/voltage accuracy, meter burden if measuring in series, source resistance, lead/contact resistance and ambient temperature.

Use a current-limited isolated low-voltage source. No mains, flyback or high-voltage source is required for this control.

### Tier-0 pass/fail

PASS: both port responses agree with conventional predictions from independently measured resistances within the declared combined uncertainty.

If not, diagnose circuit/metrology errors; do not attribute a residual to Resolutive physics.

## 3. Tier-1 required objective physical substrate

A possible experimental class is individually addressable stochastic conductive-link junctions, but no material, device stack or fabrication process has been chosen or validated here.

Each candidate must be an objectively fabricated physical switching junction. Formed links and physical contacts must be observable independently of the desired theoretical outcome.

Known possible confounds include ionic migration/redox, local electric fields, Joule heating, material inhomogeneity, history, leakage and switching-cycle variability.

Every event must be a spontaneous physical transition under applied conditions; neither firmware nor switches may calculate q and choose the event.

## 4. Freeze-ready experimental contrasts

Create pairs of candidate junctions u,w with:

- same formed degree d(u)=d(w)>0;
- one contrast Delta q=q(u)-q(w)=+1;
- a second contrast Delta q=+2;
- crossovers that reverse the physical topological assignment of q without changing candidate identity;
- sham rewiring that does not change q.

The physical attachment graph, not a schematic subdivision, defines q.

Before event acquisition verify that the established physical comparator predicts k_u^0,k_w^0 using independently measured device/field/material variables.

If the +1 and +2 contrasts cannot be realized without uncontrolled changes in candidate physics, mark T54_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE rather than fitting a topology coefficient.

## 5. Null and alternative

H0: lambda_R=0 after the independently calibrated physical comparator.

Exploratory H1 from T53: lambda_R>0 and

D(u,w)=log[(k_u/k_w)/(k_u^0/k_w^0)]
      = -lambda_R Delta q.

For Delta q=+1 and +2 the conditional prediction is D_2=2D_1<0.

Under a successful within-device q-reversal at equal baseline state, the topology-specific residual changes sign.

This is a **falsifiable phenomenological** model, not a derived fundamental coupling.

## 6. Calibration cohort versus held-out confirmation

**Calibration cohort:**
- estimate local conventional hazards/physical parameters without using held-out data;
- verify hazard stationarity or fit a predeclared alternative time-dependent hazard model;
- quantify thermal/electrical cross-talk and carryover;
- estimate lambda_R only if an independently specified model is available;
- determine sample size and fixed acceptance tolerance from the calibration measurement variance.

**Confirmation cohort:**
- new physical junctions/lots or fully independent runs;
- preassigned q contrasts and crossover order;
- blinded event adjudication;
- primary outcome: baseline-adjusted first-event log odds or a predeclared competing-risk hazard estimator;
- fixed sign and shape criteria from T53;
- no re-optimization of lambda_R;
- no dropping unfavorable samples except predeclared hardware failures.

If a calibration cohort is used to establish sample size or lambda_R, it cannot also be counted as independent confirmation.

## 7. Required negative controls

1. **Schematic subdivision:** modify only the graph drawing of a series element; predicted physical event behavior must not change.
2. **Sham rewiring:** physical operation without q change; quantify artifacts due to actuation.
3. **Device-label crossover:** swap which physical junction carries high/low q; fixed device bias must not masquerade as the graph effect.
4. **Locally matched drive:** measure local potential, leakage, temperature and power during comparison.
5. **Programmed imitation:** a software-controlled switch implementing q preference may validate telemetry but is explicitly excluded from the discovery sample.
6. **Known-physics comparator:** independently predict/measure conventional field/thermal/material effects under every topology.

## 8. Stop rules

STOP before physical confirmation if:
- node identity is representation-dependent;
- local conditions cannot be matched/modelled with adequate uncertainty;
- baseline predicts negative controls poorly;
- ordinary circuit or material physics explains the effect;
- event-hazard model is invalid without a frozen replacement;
- the sample size and error budget have not been preregistered;
- an absolute parameter value is chosen after inspecting the confirmation data.

An observed nonzero residual is **not sufficient** for a new-physics claim. It is a prompt to test additional conventional mechanisms and reproduce independently.

## 9. Required record for a later confirmation freeze

Record in advance:
- selected physical substrate, material and objective-node criteria;
- geometry of all junctions and measured electrical properties;
- full topology and q calculation;
- H0 model, parameter source and independent uncertainty;
- one fixed physical H1 model and its calibration;
- primary observable, effect size, statistical threshold and sample size;
- temperature/voltage protocol;
- crossover/sham order;
- exact failure rule;
- held-out device identifiers and data-provenance scheme.

No such freeze is claimed in T54.
