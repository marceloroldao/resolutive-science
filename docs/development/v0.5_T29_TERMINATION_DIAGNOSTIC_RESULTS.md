## Finite-size saturation

At R=8, 2R=16 while exact diameters remain 24--42. Thus the graphs have not globally saturated at the scale where universal activity first disappears.

At R=16 finite-size saturation becomes strong:
- all 128-node vertices have eccentricity <=32;
- 236/256 and 256/256 vertices do so in two 256-node cases, with the remaining case also strongly affected;
- 315--390 of 512 vertices do so.

Therefore finite size explains part of the R=16 regime, but not the onset of universal termination at R=8.

T29_M4_FINITE_SIZE_AS_R8_PRIMARY_CAUSE = REJECTED
T29_M4_FINITE_SIZE_AT_R16 = SUPPORTED

## Feedback suppression

The parent-tree counterfactual shows that previous accepted D28-A relations can remove later opportunities.

At R=8:
- N=256 seed11: parent counterfactual accepts 1; sequential accepts 0.
- N=512 seed23: parent counterfactual accepts 2; sequential accepts 0.

At R=4 additional suppression/drift is visible:
- 128/47: parent 1, sequential 0;
- 256/23: parent 3, sequential 1;
- 512/11: parent 2, sequential 0;
- 512/23: parent 7, sequential 5;
- 512/47: parent 5, sequential 1.

Feedback is therefore real, but it cannot be the sole explanation: most parent counterfactuals at R=8 are already inactive.

T29_M5_FEEDBACK_SUPPRESSION = SUPPORTED_SECONDARY
T29_M5_SOLE_TERMINATION_CAUSE = REJECTED

## Mechanism diagnosis

The universal R=8 termination has a clear immediate mechanism:

1. the allowed distance band remains highly populated;
2. graph diameter remains larger than 2R;
3. nevertheless, no pair in that band shares the exact frozen shell signature;
4. therefore no candidate reaches the symmetry-selection stage.

The frozen signature becomes increasingly discriminative as R grows because it contains the complete shell-population and shell-degree-multiset sequence through radius R. On irregular finite trees plus earlier closure edges, exact equality becomes rare.

This is a diagnosis of D28-A, not a theorem that coordinate-free multiscale dynamics is impossible.
