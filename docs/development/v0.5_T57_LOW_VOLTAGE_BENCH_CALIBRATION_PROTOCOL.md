# v0.5 T57 — Low-Voltage Classical Bench Calibration and Data Contract

**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION / BENCH NOT EXECUTED
**Parent:** T55–T56.
**Purpose:** executable conventional-circuit calibration. Not a test of lambda_R or new physics.

## 1. Fixed hardware

Use the exact T55 tree with vertices r,A,B,C,U,V,W,B1,C1,C2 and these nine resistor edges:

(r,A), (r,B), (r,C), (A,U), (B,V), (B,B1), (C,W), (C,C1), (C,C2).

Every edge receives an independently measurable nominal 1000-ohm resistor rated at least 1/4 W.

The candidates U,V,W have degree one and q=(1,2,3). Measure ports r-U, r-V and r-W separately. All non-driven branches must float, except a high-input-impedance probe across the specified off-path resistor.

Use an isolated current-limited low-voltage DC supply, ordinary voltmeter/current meter and suitable leads. No mains, flyback, HV, RF or laser equipment is needed.

## 2. Frozen conventional comparator

Measure each resistor individually with power disconnected before and after the run. **Pre-run** values alone define the independent comparator; post-run values check drift, not refitting.

Port path edges:
- U: (r,A)+(A,U)
- V: (r,B)+(B,V)
- W: (r,C)+(C,W)

For each acquisition calculate R_path from the two independently measured pre-run resistance values. At measured instantaneous circuit **port** voltage V, not merely the supply knob setting, Kirchhoff predicts I_pred=V/R_path and P_pred=V*I_pred.

Ideal nominal reference:
- 3 V, 2000 ohm: 1.5 mA and 4.5 mW.
- 6 V, 2000 ohm: 3.0 mA and 18.0 mW.

Do not require exact equality of realized path resistance across U/V/W if measured components differ within their tolerances.

## 3. Fixed off-path controls

At every port reading measure the voltage across one branch resistor that has zero ideal DC current:
- U -> B-B1;
- V -> C-C1;
- W -> B-B1.

DMM loading and leakage must be documented. If material, stop and revise the comparator in a new protocol, not after looking at residuals.

## 4. Acquisition plan

Three targets U,V,W; two nominal settings 3.0 and 6.0 V; five complete repeat blocks.

Total required port records = 3*2*5 = 30.

Before acquisition, prepare and retain a randomized order of the six target-voltage combinations inside each block. Disconnect the supply before moving probes; verify the intended floating branches.

## 5. Data schema

File: resistors.csv. Exactly one row per frozen edge; order of endpoints irrelevant.

Required columns:
a,b,R_pre_ohm,u_R_pre_ohm,R_post_ohm,u_R_post_ohm

All resistance and standard-uncertainty fields are finite and >0. Uncertainties are k=1 standard uncertainties, not unspecified accuracy limits.

File: ports.csv. Exactly 30 unique rows by (repeat,target,nominal_voltage_V).

Required columns:
repeat,target,nominal_voltage_V,measured_port_V,u_V,measured_port_I_A,u_I_A,offpath_edge,offpath_drop_V,u_offpath_V

repeat=1..5; target=U,V,W; nominal voltage=3 or 6; offpath_edge is B-B1 for U/W and C-C1 for V. Port V and I and all standard uncertainty inputs are finite >0. Off-path drop is signed finite.

Record instrument IDs, uncertainty derivation, calibration status, current-meter burden and ambient temperature in the associated lab notebook. The analyzer must never invent those facts.

## 6. Frozen statistical calibration gates

The uncertainty model assumes independent standard uncertainties for separate resistor, voltage and current observations. If material covariance is known, stop for a separately frozen covariance-aware protocol.

For path resistance and measured port voltage:

R=R1+R2;
u_R=sqrt(u_R1^2+u_R2^2);
I_pred=V/R;
u_I_pred^2=(u_V/R)^2+(V*u_R/R^2)^2;
e_I=I_obs-I_pred;
u_e=sqrt(u_I_obs^2+u_I_pred^2).

A record passes the classical current comparator when |e_I| <= 3*u_e.

Off-path gate: |V_offpath| <= 3*u_V_offpath.

Per-resistor pre/post stability gate:
|R_post-R_pre| <= 3*sqrt(u_R_pre^2+u_R_post^2).

Source setting: |V_port - V_nominal| <= 0.5 V.

These are pragmatic calibration gates, not discovery p-values; report each raw signed residual, uncertainty and individual gate result.

## 7. Provenance and software checks

CI may construct clearly labeled synthetic fixtures only to test:
1. an ideal comparator pass;
2. detection of one deliberately altered current;
3. detection of a nonzero off-path voltage;
4. missing/duplicated port records;
5. excessive resistor drift.

CI output has status SYNTHETIC_SELF_TEST, never PHYSICAL_MEASUREMENT.

When the analyzer reads user-supplied CSV it reports DATA_SUBMITTED_UNVERIFIED plus the calibration gate. It cannot authenticate measurement provenance from numerical data alone.

## 8. Interpretation and ledger

Passing means only: supplied data agree with the conventional series-resistor model within stated uncertainties.

A failure requires diagnosis of components, wiring, loading or metrology; it is not evidence for new physics. There are no stochastic link-formation events here, so lambda_R cannot be estimated.
