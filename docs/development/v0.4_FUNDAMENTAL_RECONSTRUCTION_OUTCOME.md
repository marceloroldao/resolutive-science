## 3. Core conclusion

The frozen RSMS currently defines a **state architecture**, not a unique fundamental dynamics.

More precisely:

```text
RSMS state definitions
+ generic bundle vocabulary
+ current phase representation

DO NOT UNIQUELY IMPLY

a non-conventional Master Equation.
```

At least one additional physical selection principle would be needed to determine:

- which RSMS primitives are truly dynamical;
- their domains and units;
- the internal geometry or metric;
- the structure group, if any;
- a connection/transport law, if any;
- the coupling or action;
- the observable projection.

Selecting those objects arbitrarily would manufacture a theory rather than derive one.

## 6. Scientific stop rule

The current reconstruction line shall stop before any new empirical fit unless one of the following occurs:

### Route A — specification-minimality revision

A future RSMS revision removes or demotes primitives that are not independently required and rebuilds the state from a smaller set.

### Route B — one new upstream postulate

Exactly one additional physical principle is introduced with:

1. explicit mathematical form;
2. reason independent of target residuals;
3. transformation law;
4. dimensions/units;
5. unique or tightly constrained dynamical consequence;
6. preregistered observable discriminator;
7. conventional comparator of equal complexity;
8. failure criterion.

Only after such a postulate survives its own internal audit may a new domain fit be authorized.

## 7. Preservation rule

The following shall remain preserved as negative evidence:

- failed physical-4pi derivation from the current scalar representation;
- failed `S^2` layer derivation;
- failed chirality-to-angular-4pi derivation;
- unobservable lifted sign under current fields;
- conventional equivalence of the minimal complex-scalar sector;
- failure to justify free-scalar `iota_R`/`kappa_R` as novelty;
- underdetermination of the generic connection route.

These findings shall not be rewritten as positive support in a later candidate.

## 8. Decision

```text
V0_4_FUNDAMENTAL_RECONSTRUCTION = DIAGNOSTIC_STOP_UNDERDETERMINED
```

The project should not invent further terms merely to obtain a Master Equation. A future extension must begin upstream with one independently motivated physical postulate and a falsifiable discriminator.
