# v0.5 T74 — Reflected-Diffusion Explanation of T70/T71 Sampler Bias

**Status:** POST-HOC ANALYTICAL DIAGNOSTIC  
**Parents:** T71, T72, T73.  
**Purpose:** test whether the exact local transition drift and diffusion measured in T73 quantitatively explain the one-sided pooled coordinate displacement observed in T71.

This is explicitly **not preregistered evidence** because the T71 pooled means and T73 drift measurements were already available when this diagnostic was derived. Its role is mechanistic explanation and generation of a new confirmatory prediction.

## 1. One-dimensional reflected-diffusion approximation

For one matched coordinate x centered at the exact T25 parent, approximate one accepted constrained swap by a diffusion with constant local drift mu and step variance sigma^2.

Inside the frozen symmetric matching band

x in [-delta,+delta],

with reflecting boundaries, the zero-flux stationary Fokker-Planck equation gives

p(x) proportional to exp(kappa x),

where

kappa = 2 mu / sigma^2.

The stationary mean is

E[x]
=
delta*coth(kappa*delta) - 1/kappa.

This uses only:
- the exact T73 one-step mean increment mu;
- the exact T73 one-step standard deviation sigma;
- the pre-existing T70/T71 half-width delta.

No M3/M7 quantity enters the calculation.

## 4. Dimensionless drift strength

The relevant confinement parameter is

z = kappa*delta = 2*mu*delta/sigma^2.

For A1:
- seed 1571: z = -4.63
- seed 1601: z = -4.16
- seed 1627: z = -3.89

For C_KR:
- seed 1571: z = +6.93
- seed 1601: z = +7.32
- seed 1627: z = +7.86

Therefore the reflected distribution is expected to be strongly skewed toward:
- the lower A1 boundary;
- the higher / less-negative C_KR boundary.

This is exactly the T70/T71 behavior.

## 5. Interpretation

The simple constant-coefficient reflected-diffusion approximation reproduces all six T71 pooled coordinate mean offsets with magnitude error below approximately 7.2%.

This is strong mechanistic consistency, but it is post-hoc.

The bracketing failure can be explained without invoking:
- a local inaccessible direction;
- a hard boundary at the T25 parent;
- a new graph invariant.

Instead, the uniform symmetric swap proposal combined with recursive validity induces a local accepted-step drift; confinement to the matching box converts that drift into an exponentially tilted coordinate measure.

## 6. Confirmatory next gate

A new-seed test must be frozen before execution.

For new N=4096 parents:
1. derive Stage-A bands exactly as in T70/T71;
2. exhaustively measure local accepted-step mu and sigma for A1 and C_KR;
3. predict reflected-diffusion stationary mean offsets before running constrained chains;
4. run independent constrained chains without M3/M7;
5. compare predicted vs observed pooled coordinate means.

A reasonable confirmatory gate is:
- correct sign for all six new seed-coordinate cases;
- absolute observed/predicted magnitude ratio in [0.80,1.20] for all six.

The 20% tolerance is frozen for the future confirmatory test, not retroactively applied to T74.
