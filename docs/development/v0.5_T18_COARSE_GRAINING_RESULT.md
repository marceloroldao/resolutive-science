# v0.5 T18 — Coarse-Graining Emergence Audit Result

**Source:** direct linear lift of D13-A; no prescribed connection or curvature.
**Status:** CONDITIONAL CONTINUUM BRIDGE FOUND; NONTRIVIAL GENERATOR SCALING NOT YET FIXED.

## 1. Exact structural result

For the D13-A linear lift on a ring,

x_{k+1}=x_{k-1}+c L_n x_k,

with ring Laplacian L_n, the pair state Z_k=(x_{k-1},x_k) evolves by

M_n = [[0,I],[I,c L_n]].

The low Fourier subspaces used for coarse graining are exact invariant subspaces of L_n. Therefore the Galerkin projection residual is zero in exact arithmetic (up to floating-point error in implementation). This is stronger than merely observing a decreasing numerical residual.

## 2. One-step obstruction

M_n is not near the identity even when c is small because at c=0 it is the swap matrix

M_0=[[0,I],[I,0]].

Therefore a one-update identification

M_n ~= I + delta G_eff

is not justified.

This is a useful negative result: the primitive update index k cannot simply be reinterpreted as an infinitesimal continuum time step.

## 3. Two-step structure

Squaring gives exactly

M_n^2 = [[I, c L_n],[c L_n, I+c^2 L_n^2]].

Hence

M_n^2-I = [[0,cL_n],[cL_n,c^2L_n^2]].

For long-wavelength Fourier modes, the Laplacian eigenvalue is

lambda_j(n) = -4 sin^2(pi j/n).

At fixed mode index j and n -> infinity,

lambda_j(n) ~ -4 pi^2 j^2/n^2.

Therefore on the retained low-frequency sector,

M_n^2 -> I.

A near-identity coarse evolution appears naturally only after grouping two primitive updates and taking a long-wavelength/large-n scaling limit.

## 4. Effective generator candidate

The leading two-step defect is

M_n^2-I ~= [[0,cL_n],[cL_n,0]].

Because L_n on fixed low modes scales as n^-2, a nonzero continuum generator requires an explicit scaling of the coarse evolution parameter with n^-2 (equivalently a diffusive/long-wavelength scaling convention) or a corresponding scaling of coupling.

Without that scaling, the effective generator tends to zero.

Thus T18 does NOT yet derive a unique physical G_eff.

## 7. Consequence

T18 finds a genuine mathematical bridge, but also a constraint: the continuum limit cannot be obtained by simply shrinking the primitive update. The reversible D13-A rule contains a built-in two-step structure. A continuum description, if physically relevant, must coarse-grain that structure and specify how relational clock resolution scales with relational wavelength/system size.

This is notable because no 2pi/4pi periodicity or legacy Resolutive constant was inserted. The factor of two here is purely the algebraic second-order/swap structure of D13-A and must not be conflated with the old 4pi hypothesis.
