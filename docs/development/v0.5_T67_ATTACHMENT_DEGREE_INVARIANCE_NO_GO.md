# v0.5 T67 — Exact Attachment-Degree Invariance Under Child-Quota Preservation

**Status:** ANALYTICAL NO-GO / EXACT COMBINATORIAL RESULT  
**Parents:** T61–T66.  
**Purpose:** determine whether the degree of the parent at attachment can explain the T47 residual independently of final degree sequence.

## 1. Setup

Consider any recursive rooted tree built in birth order 0,1,...,N-1 with the initial edge 0--1 and, for every v>=2, one parent p(v)<v.

Let d_f(u) be the final degree of vertex u.

Let h_k count sampled attachment events v=2,...,N-1 for which the chosen parent had current degree k immediately before attachment.

## 2. Exact identity

For every non-root vertex u>0 with final degree d_f(u), u has exactly d_f(u)-1 children.

Its successive child attachments occur when its current degree is

1,2,...,d_f(u)-1,

once each.

The root u=0 has d_f(0) children in total, but the first child v=1 is the fixed initialization edge and is excluded from sampled T25 attachment events. Its remaining child attachments likewise occur at current degrees

1,2,...,d_f(0)-1,

once each.

Therefore every vertex with final degree at least k+1 contributes exactly one sampled attachment event at pre-attachment degree k.

Hence

h_k
=
#{u : d_f(u) >= k+1}.

This is exact at finite N.

## 3. Consequence for T47/T64–T66 nulls

The T47 family preserves the complete labeled final degree sequence exactly.

Therefore it preserves h_k exactly for every k.

The full marginal histogram of parent degree at attachment is thus invariant under all child-quota-preserving parent swaps.

It cannot explain any M3/M7 difference inside that null family.

## 4. Connection to the T61 stationary equations

Divide the exact identity by N and take N large.

If p_j is the final degree fraction,

s_k = sum_{j>=k+1} p_j.

This is precisely the telescoping consequence of the T61 stationary flow equations

p_1=1-s_1,
p_k=s_{k-1}-s_k,
p_K=s_{K-1}.

Thus the T61 parent-selection probabilities are not an additional independent higher-order observable once the final degree law is fixed; their marginal distribution is encoded by the degree sequence.

## 5. What remains variable

Although the marginal h_k is fixed, a quota-preserving parent swap can alter which child birth time is paired with:
- pre-attachment parent degree k;
- parent birth rank;
- parent-child age ratio R.

Therefore the next independent object must be a **joint** statistic, not another marginal.

The lowest-order generator-derived joint moment is

J_1 = E[K_pre * R],

or equivalently the covariance between pre-attachment parent degree K_pre and parent-child age ratio R.

## 6. Exact theoretical T25 value from T62

T62 supplies class-selection terms T_k(r), where T_k(0)=s_k and T_k(1) is the contribution of pre-degree class k to E[R].

Therefore

E[K_pre] = sum_k k T_k(0),

E[R] = sum_k T_k(1),

E[K_pre R] = sum_k k T_k(1).

Using the frozen T61/T62 fixed point gives approximately:

E[K_pre] = 1.245991693056,
E[R] = 0.723491463502,
E[K_pre R] = 0.840923670634,

so

Cov(K_pre,R)
=
E[K_pre R]-E[K_pre]E[R]
=
-0.060540682885.

The negative sign means higher pre-attachment degree is associated with older relative parents.

This is a graph-process prediction, not physical time.

## 7. Decision

Do not test the marginal attachment-degree histogram as a new discriminator; it is exactly degree-sequence invariant.

The next admissible test is the joint attachment-state / age statistic, frozen before M3/M7 inspection.
