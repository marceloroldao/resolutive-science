## 1. Purpose

`lambda(k) ~ C (ik)^r` or, in magnitude, `|lambda(k)| ~ C |k|^z_R`.

T22 asks what continuum differential equation follows when the D13-A second-order reversible update is coarse-grained with the scaling selected by that spectrum.

No physical spacetime, metric, absolute time, energy, hbar, 2pi/4pi rule or legacy Resolutive constant is assumed.

## 2. Starting discrete law

Use the linear lifted D13-A family

`x_{k+1} = x_{k-1} + c L_a x_k`,

where `a` is relational resolution and `L_a` is a declared relational interaction operator.

Rewrite as

`x_{k+1} - x_{k-1} = c L_a x_k`.

The update label `k` is ordering only.

## 3. Why the two-step map matters

T18 showed that the one-step lifted map has a swap sector and does not approach identity. The two-step map does approach identity on low modes after the appropriate scaling.

Equivalently, for a slowly varying branch we may introduce a coarse relational clock coordinate `tau_R` only after selecting a clock/readout and a step scale `Delta tau_R(a)`.

For smooth coarse fields,

`q(tau+Delta tau)-q(tau-Delta tau) = 2 Delta tau partial_tau q + O(Delta tau^3)`.

Therefore the D13-A difference law yields, conditionally,

`2 Delta tau_R partial_tau q = c L_a q + higher-order terms`.

## 4. Spatial/relational continuum scaling

Suppose the interaction operator has a continuum expansion

`L_a = a^z K_z + o(a^z)`

on the selected long-wavelength sector, where `K_z` is the limiting differential/pseudodifferential operator.

Choose

`Delta tau_R = kappa a^z`.

Then

`partial_tau q = (c/(2 kappa)) K_z q`

in the formal continuum limit.

This is the central T22 result.

The coefficient `c/(2 kappa)` is not yet a measured physical constant; it depends on relational coupling and clock calibration.

## 5. z_R = 1 class — antisymmetric transport

For a nearest-neighbor antisymmetric operator

`(L_a q)_j = q_{j+1} - q_{j-1}`

with physical/coarse coordinate `xi = j a`,

`L_a q = 2 a partial_xi q + O(a^3)`.

Thus `z_R=1`, `K_1 = 2 partial_xi`.

With `Delta tau_R = kappa a`,

`partial_tau q = (c/kappa) partial_xi q`.

Up to sign convention this is an advection/transport equation.

Frozen classification:

`z=1 -> first-order transport-like continuum sector`.

## 6. z_R = 2 class — symmetric local Laplacian

For

`(L_a q)_j = q_{j+1} - 2q_j + q_{j-1}`,

Taylor expansion gives

`L_a q = a^2 partial_xi^2 q + O(a^4)`.

Thus `z_R=2`, `K_2 = partial_xi^2`.

With `Delta tau_R = kappa a^2`,

`partial_tau q = (c/(2 kappa)) partial_xi^2 q`.

This has the mathematical form of a diffusion/heat equation.

Important: the microscopic lifted D13-A map is algebraically reversible, whereas this single-field coarse equation is dissipative-looking. Therefore coarse-graining/projection has discarded information (notably the paired reversible branch/state). This is not microscopic irreversibility.

Frozen classification:

`z=2 -> diffusion-like reduced continuum sector after information-losing projection`.

## 7. z_R = 4 class — biharmonic sector

Take a tuned/squared local operator with

`L_a q = -a^4 partial_xi^4 q + O(a^6)`

under the sign convention used in T21.

Then `z_R=4`, `K_4=-partial_xi^4`.

With `Delta tau_R = kappa a^4`,

`partial_tau q = -(c/(2 kappa)) partial_xi^4 q`.

This is a fourth-order smoothing/biharmonic-type evolution equation.

Frozen classification:

`z=4 -> fourth-order continuum sector`.

## 8. General spectral-to-field dictionary

For a relational operator with low-mode symbol

`lambda_a(k) ~ a^z sigma_z(k)`,

and clock scaling

`Delta tau_R ~ a^z`,

the coarse D13-A branch satisfies

`partial_tau q = gamma_R K_z q`,

where `K_z` has Fourier symbol `sigma_z(k)` and `gamma_R` is a clock-calibrated coupling.

Thus the continuum equation is selected by the low-spectrum interaction class, not by a universal preinserted PDE.

## 9. Fractional/nonlocal extension

This remains conditional because the exact normalization and infinite-range limit must be controlled.

## 10. Reversibility audit

The full microscopic D13-A state is `(x_{k-1},x_k)` and its update is bijective. A reduced first-order equation for one coarse field `q` generally does not retain enough information to invert the microscopic trajectory.

Therefore:

`MICRO_REVERSIBILITY != REDUCED_CONTINUUM_REVERSIBILITY`.

A faithful reversible continuum representation may require a doubled field/state, branch variable, or conjugate-like companion.

This is a major T22 constraint and motivates T23.

## 11. Conservation audit

No universal conserved scalar follows merely from `partial_tau q = gamma K_z q`.

Specific operators can conserve specific quantities. For periodic/decaying boundaries:

- derivative operators often conserve the spatial integral of q;
- Laplacian and biharmonic operators also integrate to zero under suitable boundaries;
- norms may be conserved, decrease, or grow depending on symmetry/sign of K_z.

These are operator-dependent invariants, not yet physical energy.

## 12. Relational time status

The scaling `Delta tau_R ~ a^z` fixes relative continuum calibration; the multiplicative factor `kappa` remains a clock/unit choice until tied to an operational physical clock.
