## Test / Result / Conclusion

### Growth dimension
The fitted d_g drifts upward with N and varies by seed. No stable asymptotic scaling window is established.

**Conclusion:** d_g is not frozen as an emergent dimension.

### Spectral dimension
The current finite-time estimator gives d_s around 0.92–0.95 across these runs. This is numerically more stable than d_g, but the estimator window is short and finite-size/bipartite/tree-like effects have not been fully audited.

**Conclusion:** report the value as a diagnostic only; do not identify a physical or universal dimension.

### Walk dimension
d_w lies roughly between 1.82 and 2.28 and shows seed/size variation.

**Conclusion:** no single stable d_w is established.

### Cross-estimator consistency
d_g, d_s and d_w do not agree.

## Structural observation

The generated mean degree tends to ~2. This first generator therefore produces a sparse, approximately tree-like relational structure. That property plausibly contributes to the estimator behavior, but it must not be used to retune the generator after seeing the dimension results.

## Interpretation guard

This result does not falsify emergent dimension in general. It rejects the claim that this first preregistered coordinate-free generator has already produced a stable single Resolutive Effective Dimension.

The correct outcome is negative/inconclusive, and is preserved without rescue tuning.
