# v0.4 RSMS Primitive Reconciliation

**Status:** WORKING RESULT — ISSUE #84

## Question

Does the current Master Equation reconstruction use the primitive state defined by the frozen RSMS baseline, or has it drifted into an incompatible exploratory field set?

## 1. RSMS baseline

RSMS-1.0 defines the internal resolutive state as

```text
R = (rho_R, Theta_R, iota_R, kappa_R)
```

with exactly four primitive components:

- `rho_R`: resolutive density/depth;
- `Theta_R`: resolutive phase;
- `iota_R`: resolutive inclination;
- `kappa_R`: local resolutive curvature.

RS-R015 explicitly states that the universal primitive state contains exactly these four ordered components. Chirality, orientation, observables, coherence measures, and other model-dependent quantities are derived/specialized rather than additional universal primitives.

Therefore the exploratory variables `chi` and `n in S^2` used in the early v0.4 reconstruction are not RSMS universal primitives.

Classification: **D — specification reconciliation**.

## 2. Consequence for the current reconstruction

The exploratory state

```text
(rho, Theta, chi, n)
```

cannot be presented as the RSMS primitive state without an explicit RSMS extension or a declared mapping from

```text
(iota_R, kappa_R) <-> (chi, n).
```

No such mapping is currently defined.

Therefore:

```text
chi = NOT_RSMS_PRIMITIVE
n   = NOT_RSMS_PRIMITIVE
```

and both must be removed from the universal Master Equation ledger unless later reintroduced as derived/specialized quantities with independent justification.

## 3. Status of iota_R and kappa_R

Their inclusion in RSMS gives them specification priority, not empirical validation.

The current RSMS definitions deliberately leave open:

- exact physical meaning of `iota_R`;
- admissible range/equivalences for `iota_R`;
- physical dimension of `iota_R` if it is not an angle;
- exact physical meaning of `kappa_R`;
- physical dimension and bounds of `kappa_R`;
- whether `kappa_R` is related to spacetime curvature;
- dynamics and observable projection of both variables.

Hence both remain **P — proposed foundational primitives** rather than derived physical quantities.

## 4. Novelty test

A primitive can be RSMS-native and still fail to supply new physics.

For `iota_R` or `kappa_R` to carry non-conventional content into the Master Equation, at least one must acquire all of the following before target-data fitting:

1. a frozen mathematical domain and units;
2. a physical transformation law;
3. a dynamical term or direct evolution law;
4. a coupling to the already-audited `(rho_R, phi_R)` sector or another declared structure;
5. an observable projection that changes when the variable changes;
6. a conventional baseline showing the effect is not a coordinate redefinition or an unconstrained hidden scalar.

Without these items, adding kinetic terms such as

```text
(∂ iota_R)^2
(∂ kappa_R)^2
```

would merely add conventional scalar degrees of freedom and would not establish Resolutive novelty.

## 5. Relation to RCMS

RCMS inherited the four-component RSMS state and introduced normalized phenomenological coordinates

```text
r(z), p(z), i(z), q(z)
```

and a reduced scalar

```text
S_R(z) = alpha_rho r + alpha_Theta p + alpha_iota i + alpha_kappa q.
```

RCMS itself labels this as a model-reduction ansatz, not as a derivation of how the primitive state combines in nature.

The later cosmological line is frozen historical evidence and must not be used to select the new fundamental coupling after inspecting its residuals.

Therefore the RCMS combinations cannot be promoted upstream into the Master Equation merely because they were previously implemented.

## 6. Minimal RSMS-consistent state for v0.4

The reconstruction shall distinguish two levels.

### Audited conventional baseline

```text
B0 = (rho_R, phi_R),
phi_R = Theta_R/2 mod 2pi.
```

### Unresolved RSMS extension sector


These are retained in the ledger because RSMS declares them primitive, but they are not yet admitted into the frozen dynamics.

The next question is therefore not whether to invent another field, but:

> Does either `iota_R` or `kappa_R` possess an independently motivated transformation/dynamical/observable structure that makes the full state physically inequivalent to `B0` plus two arbitrary conventional scalars?
