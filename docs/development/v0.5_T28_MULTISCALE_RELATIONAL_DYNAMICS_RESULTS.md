## Candidate ambiguity

At R=1 most candidate pairs are structurally ambiguous under the frozen invariant. Examples:

- N=128 seed11: 18 candidates; 15 unresolved; 3 accepted.
- N=256 seed47: 48 candidates; 45 unresolved; 3 accepted.
- N=512 seed23: 77 candidates; 76 unresolved; 1 accepted.
- N=512 seed47: 75 candidates; all 75 unresolved; 0 accepted.

The no-ID symmetry rule therefore materially constrains the dynamics.

## Test / Result / Conclusion

### Multiscale activity

Every run generated accepted relations at more than one scale among R=1,2,4, although activity at R=4 is not universal across seeds/sizes.

No run generated any candidate or accepted relation at R=8 or R=16.

T28_MULTISCALE_ACTIVITY = IDENTIFIED_LIMITED

### Persistent scale hierarchy

Activity does not persist through the full preregistered hierarchy. It terminates by R=8 in all nine runs.

T28_PERSISTENT_MULTISCALE_HIERARCHY = NOT_IDENTIFIED

### Symmetry blocking

The system is not completely blocked: structurally unique pairs exist and are accepted. However, large fractions of R=1 candidates are rejected because the available invariant cannot distinguish them without arbitrary labels.

T28_SYMMETRY_BLOCKED = PARTIAL

### Cyclic structure

Because the parent is a tree, every accepted new edge increases beta1 by one. Final beta1 ranges from 4 to 14.

Thus D28-A creates limited cyclic relational structure without coordinates.

### Dimension gate

The preregistered protocol required reproducible scale organization before dimensional fitting. Because activity dies by R=8 and no expanding multiscale plateau has been established, dimension fitting is not used as a rescue step.

T28_SCALING_ORGANIZATION = NOT_IDENTIFIED
T28_EFFECTIVE_DIMENSION = NOT_IDENTIFIED

## Interpretation

D28-A demonstrates that coordinate-free local structural signatures can select some nonlocal-in-parent-tree consolidations without node-ID symmetry breaking.

But this specific law does not self-sustain a hierarchy of scales. The result is therefore a useful partial positive result (limited multiscale activity) and a negative result for persistent emergent scale organization.

## Next gate

T29 should investigate why activity terminates. It should separate:

1. absence of structurally equal neighborhoods at large R;
2. absence of pairs in the allowed distance band;
3. ambiguity caused by the chosen invariant;
4. finite graph diameter/saturation;
5. feedback from earlier accepted relations.

No new generator should be introduced until those mechanisms are measured.
