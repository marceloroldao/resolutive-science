# v0.5 Resolutive Relational Ontology — Initial Specification

**Status:** PRE-DYNAMICAL / HYPOTHESIS SPECIFICATION  
**Line:** v0.5 development  
**Baseline comparator:** frozen v0.4.0 old formulation  
**Target-data fitting:** FORBIDDEN at this stage

## 1. Purpose

This document opens a new fundamental development line after the diagnostic closure of the old RSMS formulation in v0.4.0.

The new line begins from a deliberately weaker hypothesis:

> Physical quantities may be properties of relations and transitions between Resolutive states, rather than intrinsic attributes that must be assigned to an isolated state from the outset.

This is a hypothesis to be formalized and falsified. It is not a claimed law of nature.

## 2. Ontological naming policy

Resolutive Physics terminology is retained as the formal vocabulary of the specification. Every term carries an epistemic status so that ontological naming is never confused with empirical establishment.

Allowed status chain:

```text
ONTOLOGICAL
DEFINED
DERIVED
OPERATIONAL
EMPIRICALLY_TESTED
```

Additional failure/status markers:

```text
POSTULATED
UNDERDETERMINED
CONVENTIONAL_EQUIVALENT
REJECTED
RESERVED
```

A named Resolutive object may exist at `ONTOLOGICAL` status without being a new particle, field, force, observable, or physical law.

## 3. Primitive ontology v0.5-alpha

### 3.1 Resolutive Nodule — `N`

A **Resolutive Nodule** is the minimal bearer of an admissible Resolutive state.

Initial notation:

```text
N_A, N_B, ...
```

Status:

```text
RESOLUTIVE_NODULE = ONTOLOGICAL
```

No spatial size, particle identity, mass, charge, energy, frequency, layer or topology is assigned at this stage.

### 3.2 Resolutive State — `R(N)`

A **Resolutive State** is the formal state associated with a nodule:

```text
R_A = R(N_A).
```

At the initial stage the internal coordinates of `R` are intentionally unspecified. The old RSMS tuple `(rho_R,Theta_R,iota_R,kappa_R)` is not automatically imported.

Status:

```text
RESOLUTIVE_STATE = DEFINED_ABSTRACTLY
```

### 3.3 Resolutive Relation — `mathcal R_AB`

For two admissible states `R_A` and `R_B`, define a **Resolutive Relation**

```text
mathcal R_AB = mathcal R(R_A,R_B).
```

The relation is the first candidate carrier of physical content in v0.5.

No metric, energy, time interval, phase difference or distance is assumed yet.

Status:

```text
RESOLUTIVE_RELATION = DEFINED_ABSTRACTLY
```

### 3.4 Resolutive Transition — `T_AB`

A **Resolutive Transition** is an oriented admissible change

```text
T_AB : R_A -> R_B.
```

A relation and a transition are not assumed identical. `mathcal R_AB` may be symmetric while `T_AB` may be oriented; this distinction is to be tested rather than assumed.

Status:

```text
RESOLUTIVE_TRANSITION = DEFINED_ABSTRACTLY
```

### 3.5 Resolutive Trajectory — `Gamma`

A **Resolutive Trajectory** is an ordered composition of admissible transitions:

```text
Gamma = (T_01, T_12, ..., T_(n-1)n).
```

No continuum parameter or fundamental time coordinate is required by this definition.

Status:

```text
RESOLUTIVE_TRAJECTORY = DEFINED_ABSTRACTLY
```

## 4. Candidate derived concepts — not primitives

The following names are retained but are **not primitive assumptions** in v0.5-alpha.

### Resolutive Density — `rho_R`

Candidate measure of concentration, multiplicity, organization or relational occupancy. No formula is selected yet.

```text
rho_R = RESERVED / TO_BE_DERIVED_OR_OPERATIONALIZED
```

### Resolutive Coherence — `C_R`

Candidate property of compatibility/organization among relations or transitions.

```text
C_R = RESERVED / TO_BE_DEFINED
```

### Resolutive Energy — `E_R`

Candidate quantity associated with a relation or transition:

```text
E_AB ?= F(mathcal R_AB, T_AB, ...)
```

The function `F` is intentionally not chosen.

```text
E_R = HYPOTHESIS_CANDIDATE
```

Energy is therefore not declared fundamental in this specification.

### Resolutive Time — `tau_R`

Candidate ordering/measure derived from transition composition or change. No claim is made that conventional time is emergent.

```text
tau_R = HYPOTHESIS_CANDIDATE
```

### Resolutive Layer

Reserved structural term. No binary layer, 2pi/4pi closure, hierarchy or sheet structure is imported from v0.4.

```text
RESOLUTIVE_LAYER = RESERVED
```

### Resolutive Address

Candidate identifier for a state, nodule, relation or trajectory. At this stage it is an indexing concept, not a physical observable.

```text
RESOLUTIVE_ADDRESS = RESERVED
```

### ZER — Resolutive Cancellation Zone

The historical name is preserved but no mathematical object is assigned in v0.5-alpha.

```text
ZER = RESERVED
```

## 5. Minimal relational structure

The first object under investigation is

```text
N_A <---- mathcal R_AB ----> N_B
```

with associated states

```text
R_A = R(N_A)
R_B = R(N_B).
```

The initial mathematical problem is to determine the weakest axioms required for `mathcal R`.

Candidate properties to test independently:

1. identity/reflexivity;
2. orientation;
3. symmetry or asymmetry;
4. composition;
5. reversibility;
6. associativity;
7. distinguishability;
8. continuity/discreteness;
9. metricity;
10. conservation under composition.

None is accepted merely because it is mathematically convenient.

## 6. First axiomatic gates

### R1 — STATE_DISTINGUISHABILITY

Determine what it means operationally or mathematically for

```text
R_A != R_B.
```

No energy or time definition is allowed before this gate is explicit.

### R2 — RELATION_IDENTITY

Determine whether an identity relation exists:

```text
mathcal R(R,R) = I_R ?
```

and what `I_R` means.

### R3 — ORIENTATION_TEST

Determine whether

```text
mathcal R_AB = mathcal R_BA
```

or whether orientation is carried only by `T_AB`.

### R4 — COMPOSITION_TEST

For

```text
R_A -> R_B -> R_C
```

test whether a composition law

```text
T_AC = T_BC o T_AB
```

is required and whether it is unique.

### R5 — RELATIONAL_INVARIANT

Search for a scalar or structured invariant of a relation/transition that is fixed by the axioms rather than selected by fit.

Only after R5 may a candidate be considered for identification with energy, action, proper interval, coherence or another physical observable.

## 7. Explicit non-import rules

The following structures are **not assumed** in v0.5-alpha:

- physical 4pi periodicity;
- golden ratio or Fibonacci scaling;
- old RSMS primitive tuple;
- `iota_R` or `kappa_R` as fundamental coordinates;
- a complex field `Psi`;
- a U(1), SU(2) or other structure group;
- binary Resolutive layers;
- a spacetime metric;
- a Lagrangian or Hamiltonian;
- energy as a primitive;
- time as a primitive;
- ZER dynamics;
- a terrestrial base frequency;
- cosmological acceleration scale.

Any of these may re-enter only through an explicit derivation, independent postulate with falsifiable discriminator, or operational definition.

Before any target-domain fitting, every proposed law must state:

```text
Objects used:
New assumptions:
Dimensions:
Transformation rules:
Conventional/null comparator:
Observable discriminator:
Failure criterion:
Forbidden formulation data:
```

A law without a failure criterion remains ontological or mathematical, not an empirically testable physical claim.

## 10. Immediate next test

The first formal task is **R1 — STATE_DISTINGUISHABILITY**.

Question:

> What is the minimal structure needed to say that two Resolutive states are physically distinguishable without already assuming energy, time, phase, distance or a metric?

The answer must be established before defining Resolutive Energy or Resolutive Time.
