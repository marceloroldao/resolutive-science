## Purpose

This document separates prior Resolutive Science concepts into explicit scientific-status classes before any new cross-domain fit is attempted.

No item in this ledger is promoted to a fundamental physical law merely because it appeared in prior discussions, code, diagrams, analogies, or exploratory calculations.

Status classes:

- **D** — derived from already declared mathematical structure;
- **P** — postulated independent hypothesis;
- **C** — conjectural/provisional relation requiring derivation or rejection;
- **K** — computational convention or implementation choice;
- **O** — observational/descriptive result;
- **F** — falsifiable prediction with a frozen test protocol.

## Candidate mathematical core to reconstruct

The v0.4 audit should reconstruct, from source discussions and surviving code/documents, the smallest explicit mathematical chain connecting:

1. primitive state variables;
2. state-space geometry;
3. periodicity/topology assumptions;
4. field content;
5. action or evolution law;
6. equations of motion;
7. conservation laws;
8. observable projection;
9. one domain-independent parameter set;
10. domain-specific observables.

If any link cannot be supplied without adding a new independent assumption, that assumption shall be recorded as a **P** item rather than hidden inside a derivation.

## First candidate reconstruction target

The first concrete target is to determine whether the amplitude/phase field representation can support a mathematically closed action of the schematic form

\[
S = \int d^4x\,\sqrt{-g}\,\mathcal{L}(\rho,\Theta,\chi,n,\partial\rho,\partial\Theta,\partial\chi,\nabla n; g_{\mu\nu}),
\]

with a field-space kinetic sector related to

\[
\frac{(\partial \rho)^2}{4\rho}
+\frac{\rho}{4}(\partial\Theta)^2
+\frac{1}{2}(\partial\chi)^2,
\]

while keeping the interaction potential and couplings explicitly unspecified until constrained by symmetry, limiting behavior, or a separate postulate.

This expression is **not yet a validated or normative Master Equation**. At this stage it is only a reconstruction scaffold for the audit.

## Gate A — mathematical closure

Before any physical interpretation, the candidate must pass:

- explicit domains for all fields;
- dimensional homogeneity;
- differentiability/regularity assumptions;
- stability or boundedness analysis where applicable;
- topology/periodicity consistency;
- well-posed Euler-Lagrange equations;
- clear treatment of singular points such as `ρ = 0`;
- declared metric signature and covariance properties.

## Gate B — correspondence

At least one controlled limit must be derived rather than asserted. Candidate examples include:

- constant-density phase evolution;
- weak-gradient limit;
- static limit;
- homogeneous/isotropic limit;
- nonrelativistic limit.

Passing a correspondence gate means producing equations that can be compared with a known baseline. It does not mean experimental validation.

## Gate C — first cross-domain prediction

The preferred first quantitative target is galactic dynamics, but only after a unique observable map follows from the candidate dynamics.

The first RGMS test shall freeze before target inspection:

- the derived or postulated acceleration law;
- parameter count and allowed ranges;
- galaxy-quality cuts;
- stellar mass-to-light treatment;
- gas contribution treatment;
- distance/inclination nuisance handling;
- comparison baselines;
- success/failure criteria;
- held-out subset or other genuine out-of-sample target.

## Current audit conclusion

The present project contains several promising mathematical motifs but does **not yet have a demonstrated fundamental Master Equation** in the strong sense required by v0.4.

The next scientific task is therefore reconstruction and classification, not parameter fitting.

The first decisive question is whether the `4π` structure and the candidate amplitude/phase representation can be obtained from a coherent state-space/topological construction, or whether they must remain independent physical postulates. That distinction will determine the architecture of the entire v0.4 line.
