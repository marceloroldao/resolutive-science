## 2. T49 alpha is a graph-generator control parameter

T49 mixes two graph-growth choices at each birth event:

- with probability alpha, choose a minimum-degree candidate;
- with probability 1-alpha, choose uniformly among available candidates.

Alpha is dimensionless and belongs to the implemented graph generator. It is not a measured physical coupling.

No physical time, temperature, activation barrier or transition-rate scale was assigned to alpha.

## 3. Exact non-equivalence to one Gibbs / softmax coupling

Consider the smallest nontrivial candidate set containing two available vertices with degrees d1 < d2 and degree gap

Delta d = d2-d1 > 0.

Under the T49 mixture, the probability of choosing the lower-degree candidate is

P_T49(min)
= alpha + (1-alpha)/2
= (1+alpha)/2.

Now compare a Gibbs / softmax selection law

P_lambda(i) proportional to exp[-lambda d_i].

For the same two candidates,

P_lambda(min)
= 1 / (1 + exp[-lambda Delta d]).

Matching the two probabilities requires

lambda_eff(Delta d)
= (1/Delta d) ln[(1+alpha)/(1-alpha)].

Thus for any fixed 0<alpha<1,

lambda_eff(1) != lambda_eff(2) != lambda_eff(3).

The effective softmax coefficient depends on the candidate degree gap. A single lambda cannot reproduce T49 across candidate sets with different degree gaps.

Candidate sets with more than two vertices and ties add further dependence on the complete degree multiplicity pattern.

Therefore:

T49 alpha IS NOT a unique physical Boltzmann-like coupling.

## 5. Conditional Arrhenius bridge — explicitly new postulate

A physical stochastic-transition model could, as an additional hypothesis, introduce a q-dependent activation-barrier contribution

Delta F_R(u) = epsilon_R q(u),

where epsilon_R has units joule.

If ordinary transition-state / Arrhenius reasoning applies to the physical substrate, then

k_u / k_u^0 = exp[-epsilon_R q(u)/(k_B T)]

and for two candidates

D(u,w)
= - [epsilon_R/(k_B T)] Delta q.

This would identify

lambda_R(T)=epsilon_R/(k_B T).

However:

1. the Arrhenius form is established conventional statistical physics machinery for an applicable activated process;
2. the q-dependent barrier epsilon_R q is a **new Resolutive postulate**;
3. current RSMS does not derive epsilon_R;
4. T49 does not determine epsilon_R;
5. no claim is made that a memristive switching mechanism must obey this simple one-barrier model.

## 6. Additional falsifiable shape if the barrier postulate were adopted

If epsilon_R were temperature-independent over a predeclared narrow material regime, then after independent conventional baseline correction,

D(T) * T = -epsilon_R Delta q / k_B

would be temperature-independent.

Equivalently the q-slope would scale as 1/T.

This is a stronger phenomenological shape constraint than the single-temperature T53 relation, but it is not authorized as a fundamental law by the current derivation.

Any future use requires a separately frozen physical specialization and independent temperature controls.
