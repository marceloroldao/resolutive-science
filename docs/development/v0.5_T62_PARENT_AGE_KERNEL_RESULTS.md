## 5. Exact invariant under the T47 parent-swap null

A further analytical observation follows from T47's exact child-quota preservation.

For a parent assignment p(v), the finite log-age sum is

sum_{v=1}^{N-1} [-ln((p(v)+1)/(v+1))]
=
sum_v ln(v+1) - sum_v ln(p(v)+1).

A T47 parent swap preserves:
- the complete child label set {v};
- the complete multiset of parent labels {p(v)}, because each vertex's child quota is fixed.

Therefore the entire log-age sum, and hence its mean L, is **exactly invariant under every admissible T47 parent swap**.

So L cannot explain the T47 M3/M7 residual.

By contrast, positive power moments such as

mean[(p(v)+1)/(v+1)]

depend on the pairing between parent labels and child labels and are not invariant under parent swaps.

This identifies a sharper missing structural object:

**birth-order parent-child pairing / age alignment**, rather than marginal parent-age distribution.
